## A. Blocking question

**1. Low orbit.**

**Entering (XR s.39, High Orbit):** "ships within a planet's gravity well at the start of their turn may choose to enter low orbit. A ship that does so is removed from play and placed on a separate low orbit table." There is no other way in.

**Where it appears:** XR doesn't say. Remastered (p.116) does: "place the ship on the low orbit table, touching the edge furthest from the planet". **Proposed:** use Remastered's placement, since XR is silent rather than contradicting it. Ciarán to confirm.

**Leaving:**
- a ship that moves off any low-orbit edge other than the planet edge returns to high orbit, "placed touching the outer edge of the planet template" (XR);
- a ship may not enter and leave low orbit in the same turn;
- ordnance launched on the low orbit table can't leave it.

**Other rules (s.39, Fighting in Low Orbit):**
- **Table:** 45-60cm wide by 90-135cm long, with one long edge as the planet.
- **Gunnery:** right column shift; lances and nova cannons fire only on a 4+; no torpedoes or mines.
- **Falling:** ships within 45cm of the planet edge fall at the start of each player's turn: small planet 5cm, medium 8cm, large 10cm.
- **Landing:** escorts and transports that move off the planet edge voluntarily have landed (disengaging). Capital ships can't land. Any ship that falls off the planet edge is destroyed.
- **Moving:** no minimum move, and capital ships need no minimum distance before turning.
