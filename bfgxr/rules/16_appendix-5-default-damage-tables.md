# Appendix 5 - Default Damage Tables

**Critical Hits Table**

*Roll 2D6 when a hit causes critical damage (a 6 on the damage roll, not against shields). Source: BFG:XR Rulebook, p.20.*

| 2D6 | Extra Damage | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | +0 | **Dorsal armament damaged.** The ship's dorsal armament may not fire until repaired. |
| 3 | +0 | **Starboard armament damaged.** The ship's starboard armament may not fire until repaired. |
| 4 | +0 | **Port armament damaged.** The ship's port armament may not fire until repaired. |
| 5 | +0 | **Prow armament damaged.** The ship's prow armament may not fire until repaired. |
| 6 | +1 | **Engine room damaged.** The ship may not make any turns until repaired. |
| 7 | +0 | **Fire!** Each fire the ship fails to extinguish in the end phase inflicts 1 point of damage. |
| 8 | +1 | **Thrusters damaged.** Reduce speed by 10cm until repaired (not cumulative; all thruster hits must be repaired to regain full speed). |
| 9 | +0 | **Bridge smashed.** Leadership reduced by 3 (cannot be repaired). |
| 10 | +0 | **Shields collapse.** Shield strength reduced to zero (cannot be repaired). |
| 11 | +D3 | **Hull breach.** A huge gash is torn in the ship's hull. |
| 12 | +D6 | **Bulkhead collapse.** Whole compartments crumple; pray some of the ship holds together. |

**Catastrophic Damage Table**

*Roll 2D6 when a ship loses its last hit. Source: BFG:XR Rulebook, p.23.*

| 2D6 | Result |
|---|---|
| 2-6 | **Drifting hulk.** The ship is a shattered hulk; it drifts 4D6cm forward each movement phase, leaving a blast marker in contact with its base after each move. |
| 7-8 | **Burning hulk.** As a drifting hulk, but at the end of each movement phase roll again on this table (replacing this result if 2-6 is scored). |
| 9-11 | **Plasma drive overload.** Remove the ship; leave blast markers equal to half its starting hits. Every ship within 3D6cm is struck by lance shots equal to half the ship's starting damage capacity; all ordnance in the blast radius is destroyed. |
| 12 | **Warp drive implosion.** Remove the ship; leave blast markers equal to its starting hits. Every ship within 3D6cm is struck by lance shots equal to the ship's starting damage capacity; all ordnance in the blast radius is destroyed. |
