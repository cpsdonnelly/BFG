# Appendix 1 - Campaign Tables

*Rolled in the Aftermath step of a campaign turn, when making an appeal. See the Appeals rules in the Campaigns section.*

**Forces of Chaos Table**

*Source: BFG:XR Rulebook, printed p.136. Chaos faction appeal table ("Other Appeals"). Roll 2D6.*

| 2D6 | Result |
|---|---|
| 2 | At the start of your next battle, roll once on the Critical Damage table and apply the result to your commander's ship. |
| 3 | You may re-roll the dice when deciding who has the initiative in the next campaign turn. The second roll stands. |
| 4 | Nominate one capital ship in the next battle. A single enemy ship within 15cm during your end phase is attacked: roll a D6, this is the number of dice you roll to hit against the target's Armour (shields have no effect). |
| 5 | Add a Chaos Lord (with a Mark of Chaos if you wish) to your Fleet Registry. |
| 6 | Your flagship is upgraded to be a Chaos Space Marine vessel. |
| 7 | Your flagship and any ship with a Chaos Lord aboard has a +2 crew damage bonus instead of the normal +1 applied to Chaos ships. |
| 8 | One ship in your fleet embarks a squad of Chaos Terminators. They gain the normal +1 bonus to hit-and-run results even aboard a ship that is not a Chaos Space Marine vessel. |
| 9 | One ship in your fleet may be equipped with boarding torpedoes and/or Dreadclaw assault boats for no extra cost. |
| 10 | Choose a single cruiser, heavy cruiser, grand cruiser, or battleship in your fleet roster. That vessel becomes a daemonship for the rest of the campaign, at no change in cost. |
| 11 | Nominate one of your capital ships in the next game. Any enemy shooting at this ship suffers a right column shift on the Gunnery table (before any other shifts for range or Blast markers). |
| 12 | Nominate one capital ship in your fleet. In the next battle, any enemy ship within 15cm of this vessel counts as having one Blast marker in contact with its base. |

**Power of the Waaagh! Table**

*Source: BFG:XR Rulebook, printed p.136. Ork faction appeal table ("Other Appeals"). Roll 2D6.*

| 2D6 | Result |
|---|---|
| 2 | Your flagship is changed into a different random vessel of the same type (cruiser, battleship, etc). Any refits are retained and the Meks add a new one from the Xenos Refits table. Your crew loses 1 Ld for the next battle only. |
| 3 | You may add up to 1 cruiser or squadron of escorts to your roster for each system you control. Each unit added requires you to reduce the grade of 1 system: Forge to Mining, Civilized to Agri, or any other type to Uninhabited. |
| 4 | If you held the field at the end of the last scenario, nominate one capital ship for each enemy hulk you captured. The indicated ships gain either Looted Torpedoes or Extra Power Fields (your choice for each). |
| 5 | For each civilized system or hive world you control you may give one of your capital ships one Kustom Upgrade. |
| 6 | You may add 1 Ork Boss to your registry. |
| 7 | One of your ships gains a +2 crew damage bonus instead of the usual +1 applied to Ork vessels. |
| 8 | Your flagship gains Mega Armored Boarding Parties. |
| 9 | Assign extra boyz to two vessels, both of which increase their crew damage bonus to +2 instead of +1. |
| 10 | You win any dice roll-offs which are drawn during your next campaign turn and battle. |
| 11 | One uninhabited system you own becomes an agri-world system. |
| 12 | The next time you are the attacker in a battle you may nominate two systems to be attacked. The battle will be 1,500 to 2,000 points. If you win, you claim both systems; if your opponent wins, they claim one neutral system. |

**Space Marine Support Table**

*Source: BFG:XR Rulebook, printed p.137. Imperial Navy faction appeal table ("Other Appeals"). Roll 2D6.*

| 2D6 | Result |
|---|---|
| 2 | You may include one Space Marine battle barge in your next game for free, provided you have a model for it (if not, roll again). No refits or upgrades. |
| 3 | You may include two strike cruisers in your next game for free, provided you have models for them (if not, roll again). No refits or upgrades. |
| 4 | You may include one strike cruiser in your next game for free, provided you have a model for it (if not, roll again). No refits or upgrades. |
| 5 | Two companies of Space Marines are stationed aboard two ships of your choice. Treat those ships as Space Marine vessels for all boarding actions and hit-and-run raids. |
| 6 | A company of Space Marines is stationed aboard one ship of your choice. Treat that ship as a Space Marine vessel for all boarding actions and hit-and-run raids. |
| 7 | One ship of your choice (must be capable of launching attack craft) is supplied with Thunderhawk Gunships. It must halve the strength of its launch bays and may only launch Thunderhawks (see the Space Marine faction rules). These gain a +1 bonus to hit-and-run results, as if launched from a Space Marine vessel. |
| 8 | One ship of your choice (must be capable of launching torpedoes) gains boarding torpedoes in addition to its normal torpedoes. These gain a +1 bonus to hit-and-run results, as if launched from a Space Marine vessel. |
| 9 | Your flagship gains a Terminator Honor Guard and may conduct teleport attacks against targets with more remaining hits than itself. |
| 10 | You may train one ship's crew after each game (mark it on your fleet registry). Trained ships may re-roll the dice in a boarding action. |
| 11 | In your next game your opponent must reduce his points value by D6×30. |
| 12 | In the next game where your opponent has planetary defences, their value is reduced by D6×30 points. |
