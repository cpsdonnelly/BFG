# Appendix 3 - Special Damage Tables

*Critical hits and catastrophic damage for the Ramilies Star Fort, the Fortress-Monastery, and the Chaos and Ork Space Hulks. Everything else uses Appendix 5, p.153.*

### Imperial Navy

### Star Fort / Fortress-Monastery Critical Hits Table

*Used by the Ramilies Star Fort (Imperial Navy) and the Fortress-Monastery (Space Marines). Roll 2D6; results apply to the quadrant hit unless stated. Source: Imperium p.88 / p.147.*

| 2D6 | Extra Damage | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 2-3 | +0 | **Lances damaged.** The quadrant's lance array is offline until repaired. |
| 4 | +0 | **Macrobatteries damaged.** The quadrant's batteries fire at half strength until repaired. |
| 5 | +0 | **Launch bays damaged.** The quadrant cannot launch ordnance until repaired. |
| 6 | +1 | **Reactors damaged.** The quadrant's shields and turrets are at half strength until repaired. |
| 7 | +0 | **Fire!** Roll to extinguish in the end phase; if not put out it causes 1 extra damage and keeps burning. |
| 8 | +D3 | **Hull breach.** A gash is torn in the quadrant's hull. |
| 9 | +0 | **Command tower destroyed.** Leadership is reduced by 2 (cannot be repaired). |
| 10 | +0 | **Shields collapse.** The quadrant's shield strength drops to zero (cannot be repaired). |
| 11 | +D3 | **Basilica breached.** All quadrants suffer the +D3 damage and any Basilica weapons are lost (cannot be repaired). |
| 12 | +D6 | **Reactor struck.** All quadrants suffer the +D6 damage. Roll again and apply the result to the appropriate quadrant. |

**Star Fort / Fortress-Monastery Catastrophic Damage Table**

*Roll D6 when all quadrants reach 0 hits. Source: Imperium p.88 / p.147.*

| D6 | Result |
|---|---|
| 1-2 | **Wrecked.** The station stops functioning; venting gas and debris block line of sight across it, and moving through it counts as an asteroid field. |
| 3 | **Structural collapse.** Replace the model with a 15cm diameter asteroid field. |
| 4-5 | **Hyper-plasma meltdown.** Resolve 8 lance shots against all ships within 4D6cm and remove all ordnance in range; everything else on the table suffers a solar flare centred on the fort. Replace the fort with 2D6 blast markers. |
| 6 | **Warp field implosion.** Resolve 4 lance shots against all ships and ordnance within 4D6cm, then replace the station with a warp rift. All remaining ships and ordnance markers are drawn 15cm toward the rift's centre and are affected as per the Celestial Phenomena rules if they enter it. |

### Space Marines

The Fortress-Monastery uses the shared **Star Fort / Fortress-Monastery Critical Hits Table** and **Catastrophic Damage Table** (p.150).

### Chaos

**Space Hulk Critical Hits Table**

*Used by the Chaos Space Hulk and the Ork Space Hulk. Roll D6 when a hit would be a critical (re-roll any result that cannot be applied). Source: Imperium p.45 / Xenos p.70.*

| D6 | Result |
|---|---|
| 1-2 | **Target high-energy systems.** Roll again: 1-2 reduce Turrets by 1; 3-4 reduce Shields by 1; 5-6 reduce all lance strengths by 1. |
| 3-4 | **Target weapons clusters.** Attacker picks one applicable option: -2 firepower to a gunnery weapon facing the attacker; -2 strength to a torpedo salvo (a *launcha*, for Orks) facing the attacker; or -2 launch capacity to a launch bay. |
| 5+ | **Target thruster assemblies.** Note each time this is rolled. Thereafter, each time the hulk wants to turn it must roll higher on a D6 than its number of thruster-assembly hits (the free gravity-well turn is exempt). Maximum 6 such hits. |

### Orks

The Ork Space Hulk uses the shared **Space Hulk Critical Hits Table** (p.151).
