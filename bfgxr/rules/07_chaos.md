# Chaos (Imperium p.23-53)

**Faction Rules**

*Source: Ships of the Imperium, "Chaos Special Rules," printed pp.19-22 (daemonships pp.21-22, Marks p.19).* **Boarding.** All Chaos ships have a +1 crew damage bonus. **Anointed Munitions.** On purchase, roll a D3 at game start for an extra torpedo type, in addition to any others:

| D3 | Torpedo Type |
|---|---|
| 1 | Seeking Torpedoes\* |
| 2 | Melta Torpedoes\* |
| 3 | Vortex Torpedoes\* |

\*Usable only once per game.

**Marks of Chaos.** One per capital ship; a squadron may only mix same-mark or unmarked ships. Costs (from the fleet lists): Chaos Undivided 25 pts, every other Mark 15 pts each.

**Chaos Undivided** (Warmaster or Lord only): once per game, before a Leadership test or command check, the whole fleet may use the marked ship's Leadership until the end of the turn.

**Khorne:** the marked ship always doubles its troop rating.

**Nurgle:** the marked ship gains +1 hit.

**Slaanesh:** all enemy ships and squadrons within 15cm suffer -1Ld (not cumulative across marked ships).

**Tzeentch:** the marked ship may re-roll any result it suffers or inflicts on the Critical Hits table, including from hit-and-run raids; the second result stands, even if worse.

**Chaos Space Marines** (taken by capital ships; uses the Space Marine faction rules):

**Veterans of the Long War:** use the Astartes column of the Leadership Tables (Appendix 4, p.154); a Warmaster or Lord instead adds +1 to a fixed Leadership (max 10).

**Champions of the Dark Gods:** use the Space Marine boarding, hit-and-run, planetary-assault, and teleport-assault rules (battleships count as battle barges, other ships as strike cruisers; the honour guard uses Chaos Terminators). This replaces the normal Chaos crew bonus.

**Chaos Terminators:** a teleport attack rolls 3D6 and chooses two results (both keep the +1 CSM hit-and-run bonus).

**Stormbird Gunships:** multi-role attack craft (fighter / bomber / assault boat) with a 4+ resilient save (speed 20cm), replacing attack craft at half launch capacity.

**Daemonships.** Where its fleet list allows, a Chaos fleet may upgrade capital ships to daemonships, one per full 1000pts in the fleet. An upgraded ship keeps its normal profile and refits but must operate alone and may never be squadroned. Upgrade cost by size: Battleship +50, Grand Cruiser +30, Heavy Cruiser or Cruiser +25.

**Daemonic Crew:** cannot embark a Chaos Lord or Warmaster, and cannot be given a Chaos Space Marine crew.

**Immaterial Predator:** may never score assault points or take an Exterminatus armament.

**Chariot of the Gods:** may take one Mark of Chaos, bought as normal, but the Mark cannot affect enemies while the ship is spectral or haunting.

**In campaigns:** does not put in for repairs between battles; it regains lost hits only when returning from haunting (below).

**Phantoms of the Warp:** all daemonships begin off the table and enter play only by warp translation.

**Warp Translation:** on any of your turns, at the end of the movement phase, pick any capital ship on the table (friend or foe) and roll a D6. On 2+ the daemonship arrives within 20cm of it facing any way; on a fail it stays in the Warp another turn. It arrives spectral, and ignores ordnance and celestial phenomena as it is placed.

**Spectral daemonships:** only partly real. Enemy vessels within 15cm suffer -1Ld at all times (not cumulative between daemonships). Otherwise it can neither affect the enemy (including with its Mark) nor be harmed or interacted with in any way, including by fire criticals: no ordnance, mines, special orders, celestial effects, or repairs, only movement and turning. It may remain spectral as long as desired.

**Materializing:** at the end of any movement phase after it arrives, and never while in base contact with an enemy, it may become solid with no roll. It then takes a Leadership test; on a fail its armament works at half effectiveness until the end of the turn. It may use no special orders until the start of its next turn except Brace for Impact. Once solid it is a normal vessel, affected by ordnance and phenomena it contacts.

**Haunting:** on any of your turns, at the end of movement, a daemonship (spectral or solid) may return to the Warp; it is removed like a disengaged ship and may re-enter later by warp translation. While haunting it is again vulnerable to fire criticals, but repairs critical damage on 4+ rather than only 6. If it returns with fewer than its starting hits, roll a D6, adding +1 if it is a battleship and +1 per full turn spent haunting: 1-3 no effect; 4-5 regain 1 hit; 6 regain 2 hits (never above its starting maximum). A daemonship crippled at any point counts as crippled for victory points whatever its final hits. It may use Reload Ordnance and Brace for Impact in the Warp with no Leadership bonus to the enemy, and otherwise counts as disengaged, including for victory points if the game ends while it is haunting.

**Arcs:** each bracket is one weapon mount at the listed FP or S. (L), (R) = two mounts, one per side, firing separately. (L/F/R) = one mount that can fire into any of those arcs, at one target.

### Chaos Ship Special Rules

#### The Planet Killer (Imperium p.24)

Ponderous (no Come to New Heading). A one-of-a-kind vessel, usually the objective of a special scenario rather than a general-engagement ship. The scenario setups (Raiders, Surprise Attack, Convoy, Exterminatus) are in Planet Killer Scenarios, under Terrain & Scenarios.

*Armageddon Gun* (90cm, Front): fires only straight ahead along the ship's spine, and must be charged before firing. It cannot fire while crippled or while on All Ahead Full, Burn Retros, or Brace for Impact.

**Firing:** place the nova cannon template touching the prow and move it 90cm directly ahead. A ship whose base the central hole passes over takes D6 automatic hits; any other ship the template crosses takes 1 hit. Shields and defences apply as normal; all ordnance the template touches is removed.

**Blocked fire:** it cannot fire through obstacles or celestial phenomena that block line of sight (planets, moons, asteroid fields). If the template contacts one it stops there and D6 blast markers are placed at the contact point (the obstacle survives).

**Recharge:** reload with Reload Ordnance orders. On a double 6 it malfunctions (a critical hit on the Planet Killer, and the gun is useless for the rest of the battle); on any other double it may fire only once more, then shuts down for the battle. Torpedoes and the gun can be reloaded on one command check (this does not remove the malfunction risk).

#### Chaos Space Hulk (Imperium p.44-45)

A Chaos fleet of any size may include one, and it must be the flagship. It cannot be fielded alongside the Planet Killer, an activated Blackstone Fortress, or any other must-be-flagship ship (Terminus Est, Scion of Prospero, and so on), and it may not take Chaos Marks (other ships still can). It counts as one of the fleet's battleships, and a fleet with one may include at most two more battleships. No minimum-points or ship prerequisite; it may be taken alone or with only escorts (cruiser ratios for adding battleships alongside it are covered under fleet-building).

**Leadership & orders:** -1 to starting Leadership, applied after all other modifiers (including an embarked Lord/Warmaster or Chaos Space Marines). May not use Burn Retros or Come to New Heading. May use All Ahead Full only to start a ram (no extra speed); as a defense it rolls 3D6 for the Ld check to ram a non-defense unit.

**Ordnance:** doubles its launch-bay strength for the fleet's launch limit.

**Movement:** moves exactly 10cm straight each turn, never slowed by blast markers or damage. May turn up to 45° only at the end of the move and only every second movement phase.

**Gravity wells:** if it ends its move inside a planet's or moon's gravity well it may make one 45° turn toward that body, even if it already turned this phase. It also creates its own gravity well reaching 5cm from its base, affecting other ships like a planet's; ships may take a stationary orbit and move with the hulk until they break orbit.

**Damage:** counts as a defense for gunnery and ramming, but is not a support or planetary-defence unit. Never crippled; it functions until completely destroyed.

**Critical hits:** use the Chaos Space Hulk Critical Hits table (p.153), not the normal table; results cannot be repaired, and no characteristic drops below zero (apply as much of each penalty as possible).

**Catastrophic damage:** at 0 damage, roll on the Catastrophic Damage table: drifting and blazing hulk results are unchanged (including any required movement); a plasma drive overload tears it apart into an asteroid field D3x5cm by D3x5cm (no damage to nearby ships); a warp drive implosion replaces it with a warp rift (ships caught are affected immediately).

#### Scion of Prospero, Thousand Sons Battle Barge (Imperium p.47)

Ponderous (no Come to New Heading). Unique: at most one per fleet, and it must be the flagship with a Warmaster aboard (a Chaos Lord instead if the Planet Killer is also present). It cannot join a fleet containing Mark of Nurgle vessels unless Abaddon leads the fleet.

**Chosen of Tzeentch:** carries the Mark of Tzeentch for free and may take no other Mark.

**Vortex of Chaos:** at the end of each movement phase, place a blast marker in contact with every other vessel, friend or foe, within 15cm.

**Vagaries of Fate:** comes with one free re-roll, usable only for its own command checks. Whenever that re-roll, or any re-roll made by its embarked commander, is failed, the ship suffers 1 point of damage.

#### Wage of Sin, Emperor's Children Battle Barge (Imperium p.49)

Ponderous (no Come to New Heading). Unique: at most one per fleet; must be the flagship with a Warmaster aboard (a Chaos Lord if the Planet Killer is present). It cannot join a fleet containing Mark of Khorne vessels unless Abaddon leads it (World Eaters and Emperor's Children enmity).

**Chosen of Slaanesh:** carries the Mark of Slaanesh for free and may take no other Mark.

**Emperor's Children Crew:** always counts as having a Chaos Space Marine crew.

#### Terminus Est, Death Guard Battle Barge (Imperium p.51)

Ponderous (no Come to New Heading). Unique: at most one per fleet; must be the flagship with a Warmaster aboard (a Chaos Lord if the Planet Killer is present). It cannot join a fleet containing Mark of Tzeentch vessels unless Abaddon leads it.

**Chosen of Nurgle:** carries the Mark of Nurgle for free (already included in its profile) and may take no other Mark.

**Death Guard Crew:** counts as having Chaos Space Marines and Chaos Terminators at no extra cost.

**Hives of Nurgle:** the side batteries (30cm FP14) work as macrobatteries; after the ship moves, you may place one blast marker anywhere along its path per functioning hive (up to 2).

**Miasma of Pestilence:** enemies firing at it from within 15cm gain no left column-shift, and all ordnance attacking it counts as passing through blast markers.

#### Slaverer, World Eaters Battle Barge (Imperium p.53)

Ponderous (no Come to New Heading). Unique: at most one per fleet. Unlike the other barges it need not be the flagship unless it is the most expensive vessel present. It cannot join a fleet containing Mark of Slaanesh vessels unless Abaddon leads it.

**Chosen of Khorne:** carries the Mark of Khorne for free and may take no other Mark.

**Berzerker Horde:** counts as having both a Chaos Space Marine crew and Chaos Terminators, and may re-roll its boarding action results (the second roll stands, even if worse).

**Improved Thrusters:** +D6cm Speed on All Ahead Full.

**Defiant Rage:** if crippled by enemy shooting, take a 3D6 Ld test. On a pass it is crippled normally; on a fail it instead gains +5cm Speed, auto-passes all leadership tests to check course when ramming, and its armaments drop to 25% effectiveness (rather than being halved). This lasts until the ship or squadron that crippled it is destroyed, after which it is treated as a normal crippled ship.

#### Warmaster Abaddon the Despoiler (Imperium p.56)

A 13th Black Crusade fleet of 1000+pts may be led by Abaddon (Ld10) for 195pts, and he must ride the Planet Killer if it is present.

**Supreme Leadership:** Ld10; the fleet may re-roll one command check or Leadership test per turn as a fleet-commander re-roll. These re-rolls cannot be saved between turns, replace any normal fleet-commander re-rolls, and no extra re-rolls may be bought for him.

**Lord of the Black Legion:** his vessel automatically has a Chaos Space Marine crew at no cost, and his retinue, the Bringers of Despair (counting as Chaos Terminators), doubles his ship's troop rating.

**"You Have Failed Me for the Last Time...":** if his re-roll is used on a command check or Leadership test and the roll fails a second time: aboard his own ship, it takes 1 point of damage; otherwise, in his next shooting phase his ship must direct at least half its firepower and lance strength at the offending ship or squadron (resolved as if it were enemy). Survivors of his wrath gain +1Ld for the rest of the game, once per ship or squadron. If the offender is out of range or arc of his weapons, it gains no bonus and may no longer benefit from Abaddon's re-rolls for the rest of the game.

## Fleet Composition (Incursion, Crusade, Renegades & God-Dedicated Fleet Lists)

The Chaos lists share the structure below; each then sets its ships and a few distinctive rules. See the Chaos Ship Special Rules for what the Marks of Chaos, daemonships, and Abaddon do.

**Shared structure:**

**Fleet commander:** 0-1 Chaos Lord or Warmaster (on the most expensive capital ship, required at 750+ pts): Chaos Lord (Ld8) 50, Chaos Warmaster (Ld9) 75; one free re-roll, up to two more (25/50).

**Secondary:** any capital ship except the commander's may be led by an Aspiring Champion (Ld +1, max 9) 30, who may buy one re-roll +15.

**Ordnance:** torpedo ships carry ordinary and boarding torpedoes and may upgrade to short-burn/guided/barrage bombs (+5 per torpedo strength) or anointed munitions (+50, rolled at game start); up to two cruisers or heavy cruisers may swap all launch bays for mine launchers (+5/bay).

**Ratios:** battleships one per three cruisers, grand cruisers one per three cruisers, heavy cruisers one per two cruisers, cruisers any number.

**Marks of Chaos:** any vessel with a Chaos Space Marine crew, or led by a Chaos Lord, Warmaster, or aspiring champion, may take one, Undivided 25 (Lord/Warmaster's ship only), Slaanesh/Nurgle/Khorne/Tzeentch 15 each. **Chaos Space Marine crews:** one capital ship per full 750 points (except battle barges and strike cruisers) may take one +35; these crews are not allies of convenience. **Imperial Renegades:** for every full 750 points the fleet may include up to six escorts and/or one cruiser (worth up to 185pts) from the Imperial Navy Bastion or Armageddon Sector lists. They count as Chaos ships in all respects and may be squadroned normally, but renegade cruisers subtract 1 from their rolled Leadership (recently turned traitor), and they do not count against the fleet's reserves allowance.

#### God-Dedicated Fleets (Imperium p.58-61)

The Pleasure Fleet of Slaanesh, Plague Fleet of Nurgle, Berzerker Fleet of Khorne, and Infinite Fleet of Tzeentch each use the 13th Black Crusade list exactly, with two changes: they cannot take the Planet Killer or the Chaos Space Hulk, and of the dedicated battleships they may take only their own god's (Slaanesh: Wage of Sin; Nurgle: Terminus Est; Khorne: Slaverer; Tzeentch: Scion of Prospero). Each also gains extra boons of its god, and a Reserves & Allies rule: the fleet neither gives nor takes reserves or allies, and always treats ships bearing the rival god's Mark as enemy vessels (Slaanesh against Khorne, Nurgle against Tzeentch).

**Pleasure Fleet of Slaanesh.** *Veil of Lust* (+20) on a Mark-of-Slaanesh capital ship: enemies firing gunnery at it suffer an automatic right column shift. *Siren's Summon* (+25, up to two Mark-of-Slaanesh ships, which may be daemonships): enemy vessels within 15cm cannot use special orders or take any Leadership test (including Brace for Impact), and a forced test auto-fails.

**Plague Fleet of Nurgle.** *Miasma of Pestilence* (+10) on a Mark-of-Nurgle capital ship: enemies within 15cm gain no left column shift against it, and all ordnance attacking it counts as passing through blast markers. *Plague Arks* (+5, up to three Mark-of-Nurgle ships): a ship that boards or is boarded by a Plague Ark suffers an automatic Plague critical hit (resolved as a Fire critical, but a ship it destroys does not keep burning and rolls no Catastrophic Damage).

**Berzerker Fleet of Khorne.** *The Berzerker Tide*: any Mark-of-Khorne ship boarding may inflict D3 extra critical hits (then no special orders until end of its next turn); any Mark-of-Khorne ship with a launch bay within 20cm of an enemy ship or ordnance may launch up to D3 extra attack-craft markers (then cannot launch next turn).

**Infinite Fleet of Tzeentch.** *Warp Beasts* (+25, up to two Mark-of-Tzeentch ships, which may be daemonships): once per game, inflict an automatic D6 damage on one enemy within 15cm, ignoring shields and defenses (braceable). *Strands of Fortune* (+25) on a commander: spend once to turn one failed re-roll for its own ship or squadron into an automatic success.
