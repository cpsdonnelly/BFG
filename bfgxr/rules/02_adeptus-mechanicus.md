# Adeptus Mechanicus (Imperium p.6-16)

**Faction Rules**

*Source: Ships of the Imperium, "Adeptus Mechanicus Special Rules", printed pp.4-5.* **Adeptus Mechanicus ships and squadrons use the following table when generating random leadership values:**

| D6 Roll | Leadership |
|---|---|
| 1 | 7 |
| 2-3 | 8 |
| 4-6 | 9 |

**Boarding & Hit-and-Run:** crews are mostly techpriests and hardwired servitors, so any attacker boarding or hit-and-running a Mechanicus ship may re-roll the result (second result stands, even if worse).

**Sacred Technology:** when taken as reserves in another fleet, any crippled Mechanicus capital ship must try to disengage whenever possible, or, if it fails, move towards the nearest table edge (squadroned ships only when at least half the squadron is crippled). A destroyed Mechanicus vessel left as a hulk may be fired on by friendly Mechanicus ships via a target priority leadership test (not by non-Mechanicus ships).

**Campaigns:** +1 Repair point per 10 renown (or part) the commander holds; +1 to appeals for refits but -1 to appeals for anything else (such as reinforcements or Space Marines). Note that refits earned via appeal use the normal Imperial Refits table (and not the Mechanicus Gifts table). Only your flagship may have multiple Gifts, as determined by the fleet commander's renown (see the Adeptus Mechanicus Promotion Table, p.209).

**Ships & refits:** Mechanicus capital ships and light cruisers use Fury Fighters (ASF, 30cm) and Starhawk Bombers (BMR, 20cm), same as Imperial Navy. Most Mechanicus hulls are refitted Imperial Navy patterns at different costs, stats, and turret counts than their Navy counterparts (extra anti-ordnance turrets are standard on Mechanicus capital ships). "Mechanicus Refits" on cruisers and light cruisers are ship-specific point options, distinct from the general Mechanicus Gifts (p.50) and Imperial Refits (p.218). The escort classes on this list share a profile with an Imperial Navy or Space Marine escort of the same name, but are exclusively Adeptus Mechanicus vessels and follow only the Mechanicus faction rules (p.49), not Navy or Space Marine rules.

**Might of the Forge Worlds.**

- *Gifts of the Omnissiah:* Mechanicus vessels roll an extra D6 when attempting to repair critical damage.

- *Tech-Priest Enginseers:* each Mechanicus capital ship may buy one upgrade from the Mechanicus Gifts table.

- *Refitted Torpedoes:* on purchase, roll a D6 at game start for an extra torpedo type (in addition to any others), as below.

| D6 | Torpedo Type |
|---|---|
| 1 | Short Burn Torpedoes |
| 2 | Barrage Bombs |
| 3 | Guided Torpedoes |
| 4 | Seeking Torpedoes\* |
| 5 | Melta Torpedoes\* |
| 6 | Vortex Torpedoes\* |

\*Usable only once per game.

**Mechanicus Gifts** (one per capital ship; the D6 column is also used by Vault of Technology to roll a random Gift):

| D6 | Cost | Gift |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Free | Emergency Energy Reserves. While crippled, reduce shields, turrets, and weapons by 25% instead of 50% (round down); Speed cannot drop below 15cm. |
| 2 | 5pts | Fleet Defence Turrets. Reduces its Turrets value by 2 but adds +1 massed turret to all allied ships within 15cm (including itself; limit +3 as normal for massed turrets). |
| 3 | 5pts | Advanced Engines. Gains the Auxiliary Power Relays and Secondary Reactors refits (see the Imperial Refits table) for free. |
| 4 | 10pts | Advanced Shields. Gains the Overloaded Shield Capacitors refit (see the Imperial Refits table) for free and imposes a right column-shift on all gunnery attacks made against it while it has any active shields. |
| 5 | 10pts | Advanced Weapons. Gains the Targeting Matrix refit (see the Imperial Refits table) for free and increases the firepower of its dorsal lance battery (if any) by +1. |
| 6 | Free | Vault of Technology. At the start of the game, roll a D6; the ship gains the corresponding Gift from this table for free. If a 6 is rolled, you may instead choose one of the above refits to apply. |

**The Quest for Knowledge:** In addition to any sub-plots rolled normally, an Adeptus Mechanicus Explorator Fleet must take a random Quest for Knowledge sub-plot from the table below. Bracketed values are the (renown gained / lost) for meeting or failing the victory condition.

| D6 | Sub-plot | Effect & victory condition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Extended Duty | Roll a D6 for each Mechanicus capital ship in your fleet. On a result of 1, the ship suffers D3 damage at the start of the game (ignoring shields, bracing, and so forth). On a result of 2, the ship suffers a -1 Ld penalty while attempting Lock On and Reload Ordnance special orders during the game. VC: you must destroy or cripple more ships in the enemy fleet than are crippled or destroyed in yours. (+1/0) |
| 2 | Xenos Logic Engine | Roll a D6 for each Mechanicus capital ship in your fleet: on a 1, 2, or 3, roll once on the Critical Damage table and apply the result to the ship at the start of the game. VC: none; the sub-plot succeeds if you win the battle and fails if you lose. (+2/-1) |
| 3 | Mimic Drive | Add a cruiser to the fleet, keeping its identity a secret. This vessel actually has all the characteristics of a conventional transport (see Conventional Transport in the Convoy Ships table, p.87) and is worth zero victory points. VC: the decoy must be fired upon. (+1/-1) |
| 4 | Sacred Vessel | Choose one Mechanicus capital ship in your fleet to represent the sacred vessel. VC: the sacred vessel must not be crippled or destroyed. (+1/-1) |
| 5 | The Prize | Nominate one enemy capital ship. VC: cripple or destroy it; if it explodes or is detonated after becoming a hulk, no renown is gained or lost. (+1/-2) |
| 6 | Experimental Ship | Pick one cruiser; roll once on each table below for an Improvement and a Side Effect. These modifications are in addition to any refits taken normally by a Mechanicus vessel. Re-roll any result that cannot be applied to the vessel. VC: it must not be crippled or destroyed. (+1/-1) |

Experimental Ship (D6 on each table):

| D6 | Improvement | Side Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | +15cm range on all direct fire weapons | All gunnery and lance firepower halved |
| 2 | +1 Shield | -2 starting Hit Points |
| 3 | Re-roll missed turret hits | -2 Turrets |
| 4 | Turn rate set to 90 degrees | Minimum turn distance +5cm |
| 5 | Friendly ships within 15cm gain +1 Ld vs enemies on special orders (max Ld10) | -5cm Speed |
| 6 | Dorsal and prow lances ignore shields on a to-hit roll of 6 | Dorsal and prow lances only hit on a to-hit roll of 6 |

**Arcs:** each bracket is one weapon mount at the listed FP or S. (L), (R) = two mounts, one per side, firing separately. (L/F/R) = one mount that can fire into any of those arcs, at one target.

**Fleet Composition (Explorator Fleet List)**

Single fleet list (*Imperium p.17*). **Fleet commander (0-1 Archmagos):** Archmagos Explorator (Ld9) 50 pts; must ride the Ark Mechanicus if it is present, otherwise any capital ship. An Archmagos is required to lead a fleet of 1000+ points (this replaces the usual 750-point commander rule), and may buy up to two fleet commander re-rolls (one 25 points, two 50 points). If your fleet is worth at least 1000 points, you may include the Ark Mechanicus Omnissiah's Victory, counting against your allowed number of battleships. **Secondary:** 0-3 Magi Explorators (Magos Explorator Ld8) 25 pts each, each assigned to a capital ship.

| Section | Allowance | Ships (pts) |
|---|---|---|
| Battleships | one per three cruisers | (0-1) Omnissiah's Victory 405, Mechanicus Emperor 390, Mechanicus Retribution 380, Mechanicus Oberon 380 |
| Cruisers (0-15) | \- | Dictator 245, Gothic 215, Lunar 215, Tyrant 215; light cruisers Endeavour 130, Endurance 130, Defiant 130 |
| Escorts | any number, squadrons of 2-6 | Nova 45, Gladius 40, Hunter 40, Firestorm 35, Sword 35, Falchion 35, Cobra 30 |

**Upgrades:** any vessel with torpedo tubes is armed with ordinary torpedoes in addition to any refitted torpedoes it has purchased; up to two cruisers may swap all launch bays for mine launchers (+5/bay); any escort squadron may take improved defences (+1 Turret per member, +5/escort). These are all Adeptus Mechanicus escorts, so they may be squadroned together normally in any mix desired. **Reserves:** A Mechanicus Explorator Fleet may take reserves from any Imperial Navy fleet list, as well as the Space Marine Dominion Fleet list (treating Battle Barges as battleships and strike cruisers as cruisers).
