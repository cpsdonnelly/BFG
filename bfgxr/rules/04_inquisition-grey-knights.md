# Inquisition & Grey Knights (Imperium p.102-107)

## Faction Rules

*Source: Ships of the Imperium, "Inquisition Special Rules", printed pp.99-101.*

**The Ordos Majoris:** the fleet's Inquisitor belongs to one Ordo (Hereticus, Xenos, or Malleus); a ship may not mix upgrades or rules from different Ordos (the embarked Inquisitor's Ordo takes precedence, otherwise the highest-Ld Inquisitor's).

**Inquisitorial Retinues:** a ship embarking a retinue may force an attacker to re-roll a boarding action or hit-and-run raid against it (second result stands; cancels out against an attacker who can also re-roll).

**Gun-Cutters:** multi-role attack craft (fighter + assault boat), 4+ resilient save (speed 20cm); ships refitted for them halve launch bay strength (round up) and cannot launch other attack craft.

**Ordo upgrades.**

- *Hereticus:* Battle Sister Detachment (+1 crew damage, +2 with Celestians), Celestian Bodyguards (re-roll teleport results, or roll 2D6 pick one with a detachment), Hellfire Sentry Guns (+2D3 troop rating when defending a boarding), Inferno Torpedoes (melta torpedoes usable unlimited times, but a torpedo-launcher critical inflicts D6 Fire results).

- *Xenos:* Deathwatch Kill-Team (honor guard; teleport roll 2D6 pick one +2), Deathwatch Terminators (honor guard; 3D6 pick two, +2 each).

- *Malleus:* Grey Knight Interceptors (honor guard; re-roll teleport results), Grey Knight Purifiers (honor guard; 2D6 pick one +2), Grey Knight Paladins (honor guard; 3D6 pick two, +2 each). Honor guards are subject to the Teleport Assault rules (p.134); a ship may take only one honor guard of any type.

**Radical Inquisitors.**

- *Hereticus:* one battleship or Inquisitorial Cruiser in the fleet may be designated as a Purgator vessel, which always counts as an Exterminator; each Fire critical it or its ordnance inflicts on an enemy causes two Fire results (extinguished separately; the bonus Fire does not itself cause further fires). Ships other than the Purgator have -2 Ld, except those embarking an Inquisitor, Elite Captain, or Sisters detachment.

- *Xenos:* may take one reserve from a Demiurg, Corsair Eldar, or Tau cruiser and buy a Xenos refit (forbidden if any Space Marines/Deathwatch are present).

- *Malleus:* a ship with an Ordo Malleus Inquisitor may take a Mark of Chaos (except Undivided) for +15 pts, and any capital ship may embark daemonhosts (+D3 crew damage but -2 Ld); no Grey Knights may be taken if any Marks of Chaos or daemonhosts are included. For the Marks of Chaos rules and costs, see the Chaos faction rules in this book (*Marks of Chaos*, in Chaos, Faction Rules).

**Grey Knights** (Chapter 666, Chamber Militant of the Ordo Malleus): use all Space Marine faction rules, but always use the **Knights of Titan** chapter tactic for free (and never benefit from Tactical Flexibility): re-roll any boarding action involving a friendly Grey Knights ship (second result stands), and Marks of Chaos cannot modify a ship's characteristics or rolls against it while it is in base contact with a Grey Knights vessel (or is one); if this removes a ship's last damage point it becomes a drifting hulk and stays so even after contact ends.

The Inquisition fields two distinct sub-lists on these pages: **Inquisitorial** vessels (commandeered Imperial Navy hulls, considered Inquisition ships in all respects, not Navy ships) and **Grey Knights** vessels (Chapter 666, which follow the Grey Knights faction rules already in the booklet, Imperium p.99-101). The Grey Knights escorts are stat-identical reprints of the standard Space Marine Nova, Gladius, and Hunter classes. Attack craft: Thunderhawks (MR, 20cm) for Grey Knights ships; Gun-Cutters (20cm) for the Inquisitorial Cruiser, a unique Inquisition craft type not covered by the standard attack-craft codes (worth its own entry when the ordnance table is built).

**Arcs:** each bracket is one weapon mount at the listed FP or S. (L), (R) = two mounts, one per side, firing separately. (L/F/R) = one mount that can fire into any of those arcs, at one target.

## Fleet Composition (Banisher List & Fleet Detachment)

The Inquisition fields the standalone Ordo Malleus Banisher Fleet, or attaches an Inquisitorial Fleet Detachment to another fleet.

#### Ordo Malleus Banisher Fleet List (Imperium p.109)

**Fleet commander (0-1 Master Daemonhunter, required at 750+ pts):** Grey Knights Grand Master (Ld10) 75 or Ordo Malleus Inquisitor Lord (Ld10) 75; one free re-roll, up to two more.

**Secondary:** 0-3 Elite Daemonhunters (Grey Knights Brother-Captain Ld9) 30, each on a capital ship or escort squadron.

| Section | Allowance | Ships (pts) |
|---|---|---|
| Battleship (0-1) | Grey Knights Battle Barge only if led by a Grand Master | Inquisitorial Battleship (varies), Grey Knights Battle Barge 430 |
| Cruisers (0-12) | max 3 Inquisitorial Cruisers unless an Inquisitor is present; Strike Cruisers need a Grey Knights commander | (0-6) Inquisitorial Cruiser 250, (0-6) Grey Knights Strike Cruiser 165 |
| Escorts | any number, squadrons of 2-6 | Grey Knights Nova 45, Gladius 40, Hunter 40 |

**Upgrades:** mine launchers (2 cruisers, +5/bay); Grey Knight Interceptors +5 or Purifiers +15; vortex torpedoes +50; Inquisitorial retinue +15. No radical-Inquisitor options (purity is paramount). **Attack rating:** 3, dropping to 2 if more than half the fleet is non-Grey Knights.

#### Inquisitorial Fleet Detachment (Imperium p.108)

Attaches to another fleet and cannot function alone; one of its Inquisitors may serve as the joined fleet's commander.

**Commanders:** 1-3 Inquisitors, each representing one of the three Ordos, Inquisitor (Ld9) 50, (0-1) Inquisitor Lord (Ld10) 75; up to two re-rolls usable by any ship in the fleet; an Inquisitorial retinue is +15. Secondary: 0-3 Elite Captains (+1 Ld, max 9) 30.

**Ordo options.** *Ordo Malleus:* Grey Knights ships as allies of convenience, (0-1) Grey Knights Battle Barge 430 (fleet must be 1000+pts) and (0-3) Grey Knights Strike Cruiser 165 (one per 500pts; they count as battleships and cruisers); Grey Knight Paladins on an Inquisitor Lord's ship +30; vortex torpedoes +15. *Ordo Hereticus:* any capital ship may carry a battle sister detachment +5 (Celestians available). *Radical Inquisitors:* extra options that restrict the fleet's choices.

**Other.** An Inquisitor's ship may swap all attack craft for gun-cutters free (reducing its launch-bay requirement); the joined fleet may take Black Ships as convoy vessels; a fleet containing an Inquisitor takes a random Secret Ploys sub-plot per Ordo represented. **Reserves:** if an Inquisitor leads the joined fleet, it may take ships from any Imperial Navy, Adeptus Mechanicus, or Space Marine list. **Attack rating:** the joined fleet's normal rating.

## Ship Special Rules

#### Black Ship (Imperium p.104)

Ponderous (no Come to New Heading).

**Gellar Field:** the ship carries an especially powerful Gellar Field to shield its cargo of untrained psykers. If it suffers a Shields Collapsed critical hit, roll a D6: on a 4+ the Gellar Field is also damaged and needs repair. If the ship leaves the table or disengages without repairing it, the vessel counts as destroyed. The Gellar Field is repaired like normal critical damage, but this does not repair the Shields Collapsed critical hit itself.

**Inquisitorial Stormtroopers:** always counts as embarking an Inquisitorial retinue for no extra cost.

**Convoy Ship:** follows the normal fleet support and convoy ship rules, but with a fixed Leadership of 9 (instead of 7) and +4D6cm Speed on All Ahead Full (instead of +3D6cm). Transport capacity 6. Cannot account for more than 50% of the fleet's total transport capacity (so the fleet normally needs at least 12 total capacity to include one).
