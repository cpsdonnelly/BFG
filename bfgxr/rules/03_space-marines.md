# Space Marines (Imperium p.135-147)

**Faction Rules**

**Boarding & Hit-and-Run:** +2 crew damage bonus when boarding, +1 to their (or their ordnance's) hit-and-run attacks, and enemy hit-and-run attacks against a Space Marine ship subtract 1.

**Planetary Assaults & Exterminatus.**

- *Drop Pods:* in assault/bombardment-point scenarios, all strike cruisers and battle barges count as transport capacity 1 for scoring.

- *Virus Bombs & Cyclotronic Warheads:* a battle barge may act as an Exterminator without removing prow armaments and succeeds at Exterminatus on a 3+ rather than 4+.

**Weapons.**

- *Bombardment Cannons:* fire as macrobatteries but always hit on 4+ regardless of Armor (even vs ordnance), and any critical from them succeeds on 4+ instead of 6.

- *Thunderhawks:* multi-role (fighter/bomber/assault boat), 4+ resilient save (speed 20cm), keeping the +1 hit-and-run bonus as assault boats.

**Chapter Tactics** (the Master of the Fleet may take one, affecting all relevant ships): Dark Age Plasma Batteries, Lightning Thrusters, Pack Hunter Protocols, Promethean Unguents, Siege Cannons, Wings of Fury, Shadow Engines, Tactical Reserves, and Heed the Machine Spirit. A fleet with no chosen tactic instead gets **Tactical Flexibility** (-10 pts on Strike Cruiser Variant refits, min 0).

**Teleport Assault:** Space Marines may teleport-attack targets with more remaining hits than the attacker. *Honor Guards* (one per ship) double teleport range: a Veteran honor guard rolls 2D6 choosing one result; a Terminator honor guard rolls 3D6 choosing two.

Space Marine attack craft are Thunderhawks (MR, 20cm; multi-role fighter/bomber/assault boat, 4+ resilient save, already in the booklet's Chapter Tactics rules) and Shark Assault Boats (AB, 30cm). "Standard or Boarding" torpedoes means the ship may fire either type as normal. Several escort classes (Firestorm, Sword, Falchion, Cobra "Rapid Strike Vessels") are reprints of the identical Imperial Navy profile and gain no Space Marine special rules; the Nova, Gladius, and Hunter classes are shared with the Mechanicus and Grey Knights sub-lists (same profile everywhere).

**Arcs:** each bracket is one weapon mount at the listed FP or S. (L), (R) = two mounts, one per side, firing separately. (L/F/R) = one mount that can fire into any of those arcs, at one target.

**Venerable Battle Barges**

These six profiles share names and stats with Imperial Navy or Chaos battleships, but are considered Space Marine vessels in all respects: they follow only the Space Marine faction rules (in the booklet) and do not use any Imperial Navy or Chaos special rules or refits from their namesakes. All are Ponderous (no Come to New Heading). Because they represent revered relic vessels, each is worth 50% of its cost in victory points if crippled, or 125% if destroyed (instead of the normal VP rules). A fleet may instead design its own venerable battle barge from an Imperial Navy or Chaos profile; see Space Marine Ship Special Rules.

### Space Marine Ship Special Rules

#### Designing a Venerable Battle Barge (Imperium p.136)

With the opponent's permission, a venerable battle barge may be custom-designed instead of using one of the six listed profiles. Guidelines: pick a non-unique Imperial Navy or Chaos battleship without an existing battle barge profile. If representing a post-Heresy ship, set Armour to 6+ and add +100pts. If its Speed is under 20cm, set it to 20cm and add +10pts. Adjust weapons: it cannot have both lances and a nova cannon; lance strength in one arc should not exceed 3-4; launch bays should have their strength halved and only launch Thunderhawks; bombardment cannon conversions use a 1:2 ratio for lances and 5:3 for macrobatteries; prow weapons can become a Thunderhawk launch bay (two bays if replacing a nova cannon); otherwise replace one dorsal weapon with a single bombardment cannon (30cm FP8 L/F/R). Try to keep at least 50% of the original armament. Keep or discard special rules as agreed with the opponent. Same VP rule as the standard profiles (50%/125%).

#### Designing a Venerable Strike Cruiser (Imperium p.137)

May be any non-unique grand cruiser, battlecruiser, or heavy cruiser from an Imperial Navy or Chaos list, at normal cost +25pts for its Space Marine crew. Considered a Space Marine vessel in all respects (follows only Space Marine faction rules); may not use its own profile's refits (its own special rules still apply), and instead uses:

**Launch Bays and Thunderhawks:** must replace all attack craft with Thunderhawks, launch bay strength halved (round up); cannot launch any other craft type.

**Strike Cruiser Prow:** may take a 6+ Prow Armour and prow torpedoes (30cm S6 F), replacing any existing prow armament. Free if the ship already has 6+ Prow Armour; otherwise +25pts.

**Torpedoes:** short-burn torpedoes +5pts, or barrage bombs +10pts; otherwise gains boarding torpedoes free.

**Nova Cannon:** a ship with a nova cannon must take the Strike Cruiser Prow to replace it, but gets a 10pt cost reduction.

- Same VP rule as venerable battle barges (50%/125%).

#### Fortress-Monastery (Imperium p.145-147)

Deployed as a high-orbit defence; follows the Fleet Support orbital-satellite rules except as modified here.

**Leadership & orders:** fixed Ld 10. May use Reload Ordnance, Lock On, and Brace for Impact. May purchase up to 3 re-rolls at +25pts each, usable only by the fortress-monastery itself, unless the Master of the Fleet is aboard (then they add to his re-rolls and the whole fleet may use them). The Master of the Fleet may always be assigned here regardless of other requirements.

**Movement:** before the game, the owning player chooses whether it rotates. A rotating fort pivots exactly 45° at the start of each movement phase, always the same direction (chosen on the first rotation); this cannot be altered by special orders and continues even if the station is destroyed. Allied ships cannot dock with a rotating fort. Otherwise it does not move at all.

**Quadrants:** weapons, hits, and defences are split into four quadrants (front/back/left/right of the station). Attacks and ordnance hit the quadrant found by tracing a line from the firer to the station's centre (or the first quadrant an ordnance marker touches). Each quadrant has its own shields; blast markers go against the model's base and affect the quadrant taking fire plus the two adjacent quadrants (unremoved markers stay in place through a rotation). Each quadrant automatically counts as massing turrets with its two neighbours, plus any docked ships (subject to the normal +3 cap); crippled/destroyed quadrants don't contribute. Nova cannons and Armageddon guns only score the D6 "direct hit" against the quadrant under the center hole.

**Damage:** all damage and criticals affect only the quadrant hit. A quadrant is crippled individually at 6 Damage Points remaining; at 0 hits it becomes an asteroid field for line-of-sight and movement purposes but stays part of the model. Damage control is rolled per quadrant. The whole fort is destroyed only when all four quadrants reach 0 hits, triggering one Catastrophic Damage roll (see the Fortress-Monastery Catastrophic Damage table, p.155). Critical hits use the Fortress-Monastery Critical Hits table (p.155) instead of the normal table.

**The Basilica:** the central weapons (Macrobatteries, Torps) cannot be targeted separately and are destroyed only when the whole station is; damage that would hit the Basilica (such as its own ordnance detonating from a critical) is instead distributed among the remaining quadrants. Basilica weapons act under every quadrant's special orders simultaneously (so they can be Locked On, Reloading, and Braced all at once); Brace for Impact still halves their firepower and strength as normal.

**Split orders:** the owning player may group quadrants into "squadrons" and issue different special orders to each group, making one command check per group.

**Boarding:** cannot be boarded by ships, only by a space hulk. Its troop rating for that fight equals the total remaining hits of all four quadrants plus the turret value of the quadrant being boarded; resulting hits apply only to that quadrant. In its own end phase, the fort may instead board any enemy ship in base contact, choosing how many quadrants participate; quadrants not participating may still fire normally.

**Teleport attacks:** each quadrant that is not crippled or destroyed allows one teleport hit-and-run attack. The fort automatically counts as having a Terminator honour guard (First Company garrison) for no extra cost, and so cannot also benefit from any other honour guard purchased for an embarked commander.

**Docking:** friendly ships in base contact may halt as if in a gravity well, and may use Burn Retros to turn without a command check. A ship docked at one of the four inter-quadrant piers gains +4 dice for damage control, and after two full turns fully docked without bracing may auto-reload ordnance without a command check. A docked ship is still targeted separately but counts as in close formation with the fort for massed turret fire.

**Ordnance limits:** the fleet's launch limit may count as the fortress-monastery's surviving bays, or be calculated normally, whichever is greater.

**Campaign acquisition:** in a campaign, only a commander of 11+ renown can gain one, either by a dedicated appeal (5+ to succeed) or by surrendering control of one forge world or hive planet in exchange.

**Campaign function:** it generates repair points like a pirate base and can be attacked like one, and it lets one capital ship or escort squadron withdrawn for full repairs return before the owning player's next game on a 4+. If found, it may be relocated: roll a D6, on a 1 it is lost in the Warp and struck from the roster, on a 2+ it relocates and must be found again before it can be attacked.

## Fleet Composition (Crusade & Dominion Fleet Lists)

Two fleet lists share the commander below. The Crusade list is a pure, insular Space Marine force; the Dominion list draws on Imperial Navy reserves.

**Fleet commander (0-1 Master of the Fleet, Ld10) 50 pts (required at 750+ pts):** must ride a battle barge, the Chapter flagship if one is present. May employ a Chapter tactic +15, buy up to three re-rolls (25/50/75), and embark a Terminator honour guard +25.

**Crusade Fleet List (Imperium p.148)**

Aggressive, insular Chapters such as the Black Templars; attack rating 3.

| Vanguard Cruisers (0-6) | \- | Space Marine Vanguard Cruiser 130 |

**Reserves:** none, and it cannot be joined by any fleet detachment except an Inquisitorial Fleet Detachment (which then uses that list's reserve options).

**Dominion Fleet List (Imperium p.149)**

Chapters with close local ties; attack rating 3, dropping to 2 if it takes any allies of convenience. **Secondary commanders:** any Imperial Navy reserve capital ship may embark a Space Marine Captain 25pts; a Captain may add a veteran honour guard +10.

**Upgrades:** up to two cruisers may swap all launch bays for mine launchers (+5/bay). **Reserves:** may take Imperial Navy vessels as reserves (per the Navy reserve rules), at one battleship or grand cruiser per three strike cruisers, one battlecruiser/cruiser/light cruiser per two strike cruisers, and no more than one nova-cannon ship per the usual limit.
