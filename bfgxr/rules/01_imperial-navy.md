# Imperial Navy (Imperium p.63-88)

Imperial Navy carriers launch Fury Fighters (ASF, 30cm) and Starhawk Bombers (BMR, 20cm); Shark assault boats (AB, 30cm) and Marauder torpedo bombers (TB, 20cm) are attack-craft options.

**Arcs:** each bracket is one weapon mount at the listed FP or S. (L), (R) = two mounts, one per side, firing separately. (L/F/R) = one mount that can fire into any of those arcs, at one target.

### Imperial Navy Ship Special Rules

#### Ramilies Star Fort (Imperium p.87)

An immense orbital fortress; follows normal ship rules except as below.

**Leadership & orders:** rolls Leadership normally but comes with one free command re-roll, and may use Reload Ordnance, Lock On, and Brace for Impact. May buy up to three more re-rolls at +25pts each, usable only by the fort itself unless a fleet commander is aboard. A fleet commander may always be assigned to it (regardless of other requirements); then the fort's purchased re-rolls add to the commander's and the whole fleet may use them, but the fort must use the commander's Leadership even if it is lower.

**Movement:** before the game the owner chooses whether it rotates (unchangeable). A rotating fort pivots exactly 45° at the start of each movement phase, always in the same direction; this cannot be altered by any order or effect and continues even if the station is destroyed, and allied ships may not dock with it. Otherwise it does not move at all.

**Quadrants:** its weapons, hits, and defences are split into four quadrants. An attack hits the quadrant found by tracing a line from the firing ship's stem to the station's centre; ordnance hits the first quadrant its marker touches. Each quadrant has its own shields; blast markers go against the matching edge of the base and affect that quadrant plus the two adjacent, and in the end phase D6 are removed from the whole model. On a rotating fort, un-removed markers stay in place and affect whatever quadrants they touch after it turns.

**Damage:** damage and criticals affect only the quadrant hit. A quadrant is crippled at 6 Damage Points and, once reduced to 0 hits, becomes an asteroid field (further hits do nothing). Damage control is per quadrant. The fort is destroyed only when all four quadrants reach 0 hits, triggering one Catastrophic Damage roll. Nova cannons and Armageddon guns score their D6 direct hit only against the quadrant under the template's centre. Hit-and-run raids affect only their target quadrant. Critical hits use the Ramilies Star Fort Critical Hits table (p.180).

**The Basilica:** the central redoubt cannot be targeted separately and is destroyed only when the whole station reaches 0 hits. If it would take damage (such as its own ordnance exploding from a critical), the opposing player distributes that damage among the remaining quadrants.

**Split orders:** quadrants may take different special orders at once; group them into squadrons and make one command check per group. Basilica weapons operate under every quadrant's orders simultaneously (so they can be Locked On, Reloading, and Braced at the same time); Brace for Impact still halves their firepower and strength.

**Boarding:** only a space hulk can board it. Its troop rating for that fight is the total remaining hits of all four quadrants plus the boarded quadrant's turret value; resulting hits apply only to that quadrant. In its own end phase it may instead board any enemy ship in base contact, choosing how many quadrants take part; the rest fire normally.

**Massing turrets:** each quadrant automatically masses turrets with its two neighbours plus any docked ships (crippled or destroyed quadrants do not contribute; the +3 cap still applies).

**Teleport attacks:** each quadrant that is not crippled or destroyed allows one teleport hit-and-run attack.

**Docking:** friendly ships in base contact may halt as if in a gravity well and may use Burn Retros to turn without a command check. A ship fully docked at one of the four inter-quadrant piers gains +4 damage-control dice, and every two complete turns docked without bracing may auto-reload ordnance without a check. A docked ship is still targeted separately but counts as in close formation with the fort for massed turret fire.

**Ordnance:** an Imperial Navy fleet may count its launch limit as the fort's surviving bays or in the normal manner, whichever is greater.

**Deployment:** deployed as a high-orbit defence, following the Fleet Support orbital-satellite rules except as modified above. With the opponent's permission it may replace a planet, with a 30cm high-orbit range from the stem of its base. Its cost is always included in the fleet list (it cannot be bought separately like other planetary defences), and it may only be fielded in a fleet of at least 2000 points; otherwise it is a normal planetary-defence choice.

**Campaign:** it can only be gained by a commander of 11+ renown via a dedicated appeal (5+ to succeed), or by surrendering control of one forge world or hive planet in exchange. It generates repair points like a pirate base and can be attacked like one, and it lets one capital ship or escort squadron withdrawn for full repairs return before the owner's next game on a 4+. If found, it may relocate: roll a D6, on a 1 it is lost in the Warp and struck from the roster, on a 2+ it relocates and must be found again before it can be attacked.

#### Lord Admiral Zaccarius Rath (Imperium p.94)

An Imperial Navy Bakka Sector fleet of 1500+pts may include Lord Admiral Rath (Ld10) for 150pts, assigned to a battleship if one is present, and to the Dominus Astra specifically if it is taken.

**Command Genius:** comes with an extra fleet-commander re-roll at no cost, for two free re-rolls in total (including the one for being an Admiral); may buy more normally.

**Inspiring Presence:** his ship adds +1 when defending against an enemy boarding action.

**The Pride of Bakka:** his ship gains one Ship Refit and one Weapon Refit from the Imperial Refits table for free, both rolled randomly before each game or campaign. In a campaign, a ship refitted this way must remain his flagship unless it is destroyed.

**The Dominus Astra:** a Rath-led fleet may include (0-1) The Dominus Astra 365, a standard Emperor class battleship in all respects (it may take normal refits in addition to Rath's), counting against the fleet's battleship allowance.

## Fleet Composition (Sector Fleet Lists)

Several sector battlefleets share the structure below; each sector then sets its own ships and ratios.

**Shared structure:**

**Fleet commander:** 0-1 Admiral. You may include up to one Admiral, who may be assigned to any ship in the fleet. If the fleet is worth at least 750 points, an Admiral must be included to lead it. For all rules purposes, the term 'Admiral' refers to all three types: Vice Admiral (Ld8) 50 points, Fleet Admiral (Ld9) 75 points, Solar Admiral (Ld10) 100 points. An Admiral comes with one fleet commander re-roll for free, and may purchase up to three additional re-rolls (one 25 points, two 50 points, three 75 points).

**Secondary:** 0-3 Strategic Commanders (+1 Ld, Max 9) 30 points each. Each strategic commander in the fleet may purchase a single re-roll for +15 points. This re-roll may only be used on their assigned vessel or squadron.

**Escorts:** any number in squadrons of 2-6.

**Ordnance:** Imperial Navy attack craft follow all the normal rules for their type, and any vessel with torpedo tubes is armed with ordinary torpedoes. Any capital ship armed with torpedoes may be equipped with either short burn torpedoes, guided torpedoes, or barrage bombs (choose only one) for +5 points per point of its torpedo strength. Alternatively, for +50 points, it may roll on the following table at the start of the game, gaining the appropriate torpedo type shown in addition to its regular torpedoes:

| D3 | Torpedo Type |
|---|---|
| 1 | Seeking Torpedoes\* |
| 2 | Melta Torpedoes\* |
| 3 | Vortex Torpedoes\* |

\*Usable only once per game.

Up to two cruisers or battlecruisers in the fleet may replace all of their launch bays with mine launchers for +5 points per bay replaced. Any capital ship in the fleet with 6+ prow armour may purchase a power ram for +5 points, which imparts an automatic hit against the target when ramming an enemy ship (in addition to any other hits scored normally). Only one nova cannon armed vessel may be included for every 500 points or part thereof in your fleet.

**Reserves:** from any Imperial Navy or Adeptus Mechanicus list.

**Battlefleet Cadia** (*Imperium p.91*): a Bastion fleet may instead be Battlefleet Cadia. *Inexperienced Crews:* every ship and squadron rolls two dice and takes the lower for Leadership. *0-5 Veteran Captains:* a special secondary commander, +1 Ld (max 10) 15pts each, may buy one re-roll +15 usable only on their own ship or squadron. You may wait until after rolling for Leadership before deciding where to assign veteran captains; their ships must be squadroned (the whole squadron gains the bonus). Mark the escort carrying the captain, and if it is destroyed the bonus is lost.

**Reserve Fleets of Segmentum Obscurus** (*Imperium p.91*): a Battlefleet Obscura reserve fleet uses the Bastion list, but its reserve choices may instead be Chaos vessels representing very old Imperial hulls: Repulsive 230, Executor 210, Hades 200, Carnage 180, Murder 170. These are picked in the normal manner and are not allies of convenience, but may take none of the refits in their profiles. *Strange Happenings:* any ship or squadron that rolls a double on a command check becomes unreliable for the rest of the game (it will not fight on once crippled and always attempts to disengage every turn, moving towards the closest point on the nearest table edge if it fails). If that command check is also failed, the ship may do nothing that turn but its minimum move directly ahead. Against Chaos fleets, an unreliable ship that rolls a second double must attempt to disengage as if crippled; if that second double is also a failed command check, the ship immediately defects to the Chaos player for the rest of the game, and victory points for it go to neither player.
