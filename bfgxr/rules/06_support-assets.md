# Support Assets (Rulebook p.61-75)

Fleet-support ships and planetary defences, available to add to fleets from any list (subject to the Fleet Support rules on Rulebook p.61-64: high- and low-orbit defences cannot use most special orders, and so on). Launch bays carry the fielding fleet's own fighters and bombers, shown generically as ASF 30cm and BMR 20cm. A fleet may include up to three Blackstone Fortresses total.

**Arcs:** each bracket is one weapon mount at the listed FP or S. (L), (R) = two mounts, one per side, firing separately. (L/F/R) = one mount that can fire into any of those arcs, at one target.

### Support Ship Special Rules

#### Blackstone Fortress (Rulebook p.68)

Represents the stations while under Imperial control: a dormant fortress with Adeptus Mechanicus weapons, launch bays, and turrets grafted on. It cannot use any of the Activated Blackstone Fortress rules. Although historically Imperial or (once stolen) Abaddon's, it may be included as a defence for any race's fleet.

#### Activated Blackstone Fortress (Rulebook p.69)

The awakened alien form (a Talisman of Vaul), having shed its grafted-on Imperial equipment.

**Orders & movement:** cannot use special orders but automatically passes any other Leadership test it must make. Moves up to 10cm in any direction each movement phase and does not need to turn.

**No crew:** its boarding result is always 0 plus any applicable bonuses (for example a higher troop rating from onboard defenses).

**Warp Cannons:** treated as lance weapons that ignore the target's shields and strike the hull directly; they also ignore holofields, reactive hull saves, and any other special defences.

**Super Mega-Death Shot:** if not crippled, it may charge a single devastating shot by not moving or firing for two turns; on the following turn it must stay still and fire in the shooting phase. Trace a straight 90cm line from its stem in any direction with the nova cannon template: any ship (friend or foe) whose stem the template touches takes 8 lance shots ignoring shields and all other defences, and any ship whose stem touches the template's centre is destroyed outright. In Exterminatus, if it reaches the low-orbit table the planet is destroyed on a 2+ when the template touches the planetary edge. Up to three Fortresses may combine the shot if one template overlaps all their bases without overlapping any participating stem (fire from the template centre); each extra Fortress adds +90cm range and +8 lance shots. Ordnance or escorts touched while charging are destroyed. A capital ship may disrupt the charge by touching the template, taking 8 unbraceable shield-ignoring lance shots per charging Fortress; if it does, each Fortress takes D6 hits, cannot move or shoot next turn, and must recharge.

#### Minefield (Rulebook p.67)

Covers a D3x5cm by D3x5cm area, placed with at least one edge within 15cm of a tabletop feature (not in a planet's gravity well); treated as an asteroid field except as follows. In the owner's ordnance phase, roll a D6 for each enemy ship within 30cm of an edge (but outside the field): on a 5+ it is detected, and the owner may place one mine marker touching the field's edge per detected ship. Roll separately for each enemy ship inside the field; on a 5+ it is immediately hit by a mine (in addition to normal asteroid-field damage). Add +1 to detection rolls against ships on All Ahead Full; subtract 1 against escorts and ships on Burn Retros (so escorts on Burn Retros cannot be detected). A minefield may be fired on like an asteroid field: while it has at least one blast marker in contact, subtract 1 from detection rolls, and in each end phase remove D6 blast markers touching it (in addition to normal removal).

#### Orbital Mine (Rulebook p.67)

Follows the normal mine rules (Rulebook p.27). Mines bought as defences start on the table within a planet's gravity well and activate as soon as an enemy ship enters the battlefield. They award the opponent victory points when their markers are removed, whether they detonated or were destroyed.
