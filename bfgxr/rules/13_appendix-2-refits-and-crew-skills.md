# Appendix 2 - Refits and Crew Skills

Refits and crew skills are shared across factions rather than belonging to any one of them, so they are printed here once. The Campaigns rules (Ship Experience, Appeals) and several faction and fleet-list rules point to these tables.

### How refits work

*Source: Imperium p.151 / Xenos p.122.*

A ship can never have or benefit from more than one copy of any named refit, no matter how it was acquired. Refits sharing a name are the same refit even when they come from different tables or sources: a ship granted **Extra Turrets** from the Imperial Refits table cannot also buy **Extra Turrets** from the Xenos Refits table.

Each refit table is subdivided into three sub-tables: Engine, Ship, and Weapon Refits. The tables are not faction-restricted; use the table found in the same book as your faction list (so Chaos ships use the Imperial Refits table).

**Standard games.** Refits are not freely available. You may only buy them where your faction rules and fleet list direct, subject to any limits those impose.

**Campaign games.** You may also acquire a refit by making an appeal in the Aftermath step of a campaign turn. See the Appeals rules in the Campaigns section.

**Roll or buy.** Normally you buy the refit, adding its points cost to the ship's cost as usual. When a refit is acquired via a campaign appeal, or by certain fleet or faction special rules, you instead roll for it: the refit's points cost is waived, but other associated costs are not (such as the points increase the appeal itself imposes).

**Rolling.** Unless directed to a specific sub-table, roll a D3 for the category (1 Engine, 2 Ship, 3 Weapon), then a D6 on that sub-table. Re-roll any result the ship cannot take, whether because it already has that refit, is prohibited from equipping it, or already has every refit in the category.

*Note: the Appeals rules in the Campaigns section reach the same result by a different roll, using a D6 for the category (1-2 Ship, 3-4 Engine, 5-6 Weapon). Both are as printed in their respective source books; the odds are identical.*

**Imperial Crew Skills**

*Roll a D6. Re-roll duplicates or results that cannot apply; a ship with all six gains no more. Source: Imperium p.151.*

| D6 | Crew Skill |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Expert Gunnery.** Lock On command checks: roll 3D6 and discard the highest. |
| 2 | **Skilled Engineers.** All Ahead Full and Burn Retros command checks: roll 3D6 and discard the highest. |
| 3 | **Adept Trimsmen.** Come to New Heading command checks: roll 3D6 and discard the highest. |
| 4 | **Excellent Pilots.** Bomber squadrons re-roll the dice determining their number of attacks; fighters gain a 4+ resilient save if they do not already have one. No effect on multi-role attack craft. |
| 5 | **Disciplined Crew.** Once per battle, re-roll a failed leadership test or command check. |
| 6 | **Elite Command Crew.** Once per battle, before making a leadership test or command check, automatically pass it without rolling. |

### Xenos Refits - Engine Refits

*The twin of the Imperial table; acquisition works identically. Entries marked (shared) are the same refit as the Imperial one of that name, so a ship cannot hold both. Source: Xenos p.123.*

| D6 | Cost | Engine Refit |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 pts | **Silent Running.** +2 Ld when attempting to disengage. If the leadership test is failed, the ship may instead move directly forward exactly 10cm, ignoring blast markers and celestial phenomena. |
| 2 | 5 pts | **Navigational Shields.** No Speed reduction for moving through blast markers (including gas and dust clouds). *(shared)* |
| 3 | 10 pts | **Auxiliary Power Relays.** +5cm Speed. *(shared)* |
| 4 | 10 pts | **Secondary Reactors.** +2D6cm extra Speed on All Ahead Full. *(shared)* |
| 5 | 20 pts | **Gravity Anchor.** Once per turn, make an extra 45° turn at the end of the move. No effect while the ship is in a gravity well. |
| 6 | 20 pts | **Jink Engines.** At the start of the shooting phase, take a leadership test; if passed, gain a 6+ save against hits from enemy direct fire weapons until the end of the turn. Cannot be used while braced. |

**Xenos Crew Skills**

*Roll a D6. Re-roll duplicates or results that cannot apply. Note that this table differs from the Imperial one at results 2, 3 and 4. Source: Xenos p.122.*

| D6 | Crew Skill |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Expert Gunnery.** Lock On command checks: roll 3D6 and discard the highest. |

| 4 | **Superior Pilots.** The ship's fighters and bombers (and any multi-role attack craft that can act as at least one of those types) gain a 4+ resilient save if they do not already have one. Those that already have a resilient save instead reduce its difficulty by 1, to a minimum of 2+. |
| 5 | **Disciplined Crew.** Once per battle, re-roll a failed leadership test or command check. |
| 6 | **Elite Command Crew.** Once per battle, before making a leadership test or command check, automatically pass it without rolling. |
