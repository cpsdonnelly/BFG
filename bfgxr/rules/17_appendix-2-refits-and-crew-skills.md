# Appendix 2 - Refits and Crew Skills

Refits and crew skills are shared across factions rather than belonging to any one of them, so they are printed here once. The Campaigns rules (Ship Experience, Appeals) and several faction and fleet-list rules point to these tables.

### How refits work

*Source: Imperium p.151 / Xenos p.122.*

A ship can never have or benefit from more than one copy of any named refit, no matter how it was acquired. Refits sharing a name are the same refit even when they come from different tables or sources: a ship granted **Extra Turrets** from the Imperial Refits table cannot also buy **Extra Turrets** from the Xenos Refits table.

Each refit table is subdivided into three sub-tables: Engine, Ship, and Weapon Refits. The tables are not faction-restricted; use the table found in the same book as your faction list (so Chaos ships use the Imperial Refits table).

**Standard games.** Refits are not freely available. You may only buy them where your faction rules and fleet list direct, subject to any limits those impose.

**Campaign games.** You may also acquire a refit by making an appeal in the Aftermath step of a campaign turn. See the Appeals rules in the Campaigns section.

**Roll or buy.** Normally you buy the refit, adding its points cost to the ship's cost as usual. When a refit is acquired via a campaign appeal, or by certain fleet or faction special rules, you instead roll for it: the refit's points cost is waived, but other associated costs are not (such as the points increase the appeal itself imposes).

**Rolling.** Unless directed to a specific sub-table, roll a D6 for the category (1-2 Ship, 3-4 Engine, 5-6 Weapon), then a D6 on that sub-table. Re-roll any result the ship cannot take, whether because it already has that refit, is prohibited from equipping it, or already has every refit in the category.

**Imperial Refits - Engine Refits**

*Source: Imperium p.152.*

| D6 | Cost | Engine Refit |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 pts | **Arrester Engines.** The ship has +1Ld while attempting command checks to go on Burn Retros or All Ahead Full special orders. |
| 2 | 5 pts | **Navigational Shields.** The ship does not suffer reductions to its speed for moving through blast markers (this includes gas and dust clouds and similar effects). |
| 3 | 10 pts | **Auxiliary Power Relays.** +5cm Speed. |
| 4 | 10 pts | **Secondary Reactors.** +2D6cm extra Speed on All Ahead Full. |
| 5 | 20 pts | **Manoeuvring Thrusters.** Reduce the distance moved before turning by 5cm. |
| 6 | 20 pts | **Evasive Jets.** At the start of the enemy shooting phase, the ship may take a leadership test. If the test is passed, the ship immediately turns 45° in the direction of your choice and cannot go on special orders during its next turn. |

**Imperial Refits - Ship Refits**

*Source: Imperium p.152.*

| D6 | Cost | Ship Refit |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 pts | **Improved Logic Engines.** The ship does not suffer -1Ld to its command checks while in contact with blast markers. |
| 2 | 5 pts | **Improved Sensor Array.** When making command checks, the ship gains +2Ld if any enemy ships are on special orders instead of only +1Ld. |
| 3 | 10 pts | **Superior Damage Control.** The ship may roll an additional D6 when attempting to repair critical damage. |
| 4 | 10 pts | **Reinforced Hull.** The ship's hull is fitted with additional armour and internal bracing, increasing its maximum damage points by 25% (rounding up) but also reducing its Speed characteristic by 5cm. Escorts cannot take this refit. |
| 5 | 20 pts | **Additional Shield Generator.** +1 Shields. |
| 6 | 20 pts | **Overloaded Shield Capacitors.** After an enemy vessel rolls to hit against the ship, roll a D6 for each of the ship's active shields. For each result of 6, discard one incoming hit. This refit has no effect against hits that bypass the target's shields. |

**Imperial Refits - Weapon Refits**

*Source: Imperium p.152.*

| D6 | Cost | Weapon Refit |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 pts | **Extra Turrets.** The ship adds +1 to its Turrets value. If the ship already has a Turrets value of 6 or higher, re-roll this result. |
| 2 | 5 pts | **Auto-loaders.** The ship has +1Ld while attempting command checks to go on Reload Ordnance special orders. Re-roll this result if the ship has no ordnance. |
| 3 | 10 pts | **Superior Fire Control.** +1 Ld on Lock On command checks. |
| 4 | 10 pts | **Turbo Weapons.** The ship does not suffer a right column-shift when firing its gunnery weapons at targets more than 30cm away. |
| 5 | 20 pts | **Targeting Matrix.** All gunnery weapons benefit from an extra left column-shift in addition to any other column-shifts that apply. |
| 6 | 20 pts | **Motion-Tracking Targeters.** If the ship is on All Ahead Full, Burn Retros, or Come to New Heading special orders, its weapons' effectiveness is reduced by only 25% (rounding up) rather than halved. |

**Imperial Crew Skills**

*Used by Adeptus Mechanicus, Chaos, Imperial Navy, Inquisition, Rogue Trader, and Space Marine vessels. Roll a D6. Re-roll duplicates or results that cannot apply; a ship with all six gains no more. Source: Imperium p.151.*

| D6 | Crew Skill |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Expert Gunnery.** When the vessel attempts a command check for Lock On special orders, roll 3D6 and discard the highest result. |
| 2 | **Skilled Engineers.** When the ship attempts a command check for All Ahead Full or Burn Retros special orders, roll 3D6 and discard the highest result. |
| 3 | **Adept Trimsmen.** When the ship attempts a command check for Come to New Heading special orders, roll 3D6 and discard the highest result. |
| 4 | **Excellent Pilots.** The vessel's bomber squadrons may re-roll the dice when determining the number of attacks they make. Additionally, the vessel's fighters gain a 4+ resilient save (if they do not already have one). This crew skill has no effect on multi-role attack craft. |
| 5 | **Disciplined Crew.** Once per battle the ship may re-roll a failed leadership test or command check. |
| 6 | **Elite Command Crew.** Once per battle before making a leadership test or command check, the ship may automatically pass the test or check without needing to roll. |

### Xenos Refits - Engine Refits

*The twin of the Imperial table; acquisition works identically. Entries marked (shared) are the same refit as the Imperial one of that name, so a ship cannot hold both. Source: Xenos p.123.*

| D6 | Cost | Engine Refit |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 pts | **Silent Running.** The ship gains +2Ld while attempting to disengage and, if it fails the leadership test, it may move directly forward exactly 10cm (ignoring blast markers and celestial phenomena). |
| 2 | 5 pts | **Navigational Shields.** The ship does not suffer reductions to its speed for moving through blast markers (this includes gas and dust clouds and similar effects). *(shared)* |
| 3 | 10 pts | **Auxiliary Power Relays.** +5cm Speed. *(shared)* |
| 4 | 10 pts | **Secondary Reactors.** The ship adds an extra +2D6cm to its speed when on All Ahead Full special orders. *(shared)* |
| 5 | 20 pts | **Gravity Anchor.** Once per turn, the ship may make an extra 45° turn at the end of its move. This refit has no effect while the ship is actually in a gravity well. |
| 6 | 20 pts | **Jink Engines.** At the start of the shooting phase, the ship may take a leadership test. If it is passed, the ship gains a 6+ saving throw against hits from enemy direct fire weapons until the end of the turn. This save cannot be used while braced. |

**Xenos Crew Skills**

*Used by Dark Eldar, Eldar, Ork, and Tau Empire vessels (Necrons and Tyranids do not gain crew skills). Roll a D6. Re-roll duplicates or results that cannot apply. Note that this table differs from the Imperial one at results 2, 3 and 4. Source: Xenos p.122.*

| D6 | Crew Skill |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Expert Gunnery.** When the vessel attempts a command check for Lock On special orders, roll 3D6 and discard the highest result. |
| 2 | **Warrior Host.** The ship may re-roll its result when fighting a boarding action, but the second result stands, even if it is worse. |
| 3 | **Strategic Analyst.** The ship may always attempt to go on special orders, even if another ship or squadron in the fleet has failed a command check on the same turn. |
| 4 | **Superior Pilots.** The ship's fighters and bombers (and any multi-role attack craft that can act as at least one of those types) gain a 4+ resilient save if they do not already have one. Those that already have a resilient save instead reduce the difficulty of their save by 1, to a minimum of 2+. |
| 5 | **Disciplined Crew.** Once per battle the ship may re-roll a failed leadership test or command check. |
| 6 | **Elite Command Crew.** Once per battle before making a leadership test or command check, the ship may automatically pass the test or check without needing to roll. |
