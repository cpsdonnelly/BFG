# Rogue Traders (Imperium p.110-129)

**Faction Rules**

*Source: Ships of the Imperium, "Rogue Trader Special Rules", printed pp.114-115.* **Fortune Rating:** the commander has a Fortune rating that works in parallel to the points limit; refits, special vessels, etc. carry a Fortune cost, and the total cannot exceed the rating. **The Warrant of Trade:** choose the dynasty's Warrant origin, which sets the base Fortune rating and special options:

| Warrant Origin | Fortune | Special Option |
|---|---|---|
| Exiled from Power | 1 | Each non-Rogue-Trader capital ship adds +1 Fortune. |
| Imperial Trade Mandate | 3 | Convoy ship Fortune increased by 50% (round down). |
| Recognition of Service | 5 | Men O' War may take no Xenos refits, but may take a third Imperial refit. |
| Cloak and Dagger Scheme | 5 | Men O' War may take no Imperial refits, but may take a third Xenos refit. |
| Ministorum Bequest | 7 | Convoy ship Fortune halved (round up). |
| Self-Promotion | 9 | Each non-Rogue-Trader capital ship costs +1 Fortune. |

**The Best That Money Can Buy.**

- *Customized Starships:* Rogue Trader ships are represented by the highly customizable Rogue Trader Man O' War.

- *Refitted Torpedoes (+20pts):* roll a D6 at game start for an extra torpedo type (in addition to any others):

| D6 | Torpedo Type |
|---|---|
| 1 | Short Burn Torpedoes |
| 2 | Barrage Bombs |
| 3 | Guided Torpedoes |
| 4 | Seeking Torpedoes\* |
| 5 | Melta Torpedoes\* |
| 6 | Vortex Torpedoes\* |

\*Usable only once per game.

**Rogue Trader Convoy Ships:** follow the normal fleet-support/convoy rules; if they disengage without scoring or counting toward victory they are treated as destroyed (crippling/destroying an enemy counts as scoring); each point of their transport capacity adds +1 Fortune.

**Business as Usual:** a fleet with any Rogue Trader convoy ships rolls on the Business as Usual table (unless convoy scoring already applies), in addition to any standard sub-plot. Bracketed values are the (renown gained / lost) for meeting or failing the victory condition.

| D6 | Sub-plot | Effect & victory condition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trooping the Colour | Your sloops, caravels, and carracks are worth double VP if crippled or destroyed. VC: at least one convoy ship survives and the enemy scores no more than 10% of their points from your convoy ships. (+2/-1) |
| 2 | Punitive Strike | Enemy direct fire must target a convoy ship if able, unless it passes a target priority test. VC: at least half your transport capacity survives. (+1/-1) |
| 3 | Colony Settlement | A convoy ship destroyed by boarding or hit-and-run gives the enemy 100% of its VP; destroyed any other way, whoever caused it loses 100% of its VP. VC: at least one convoy ship survives and the enemy does not hold the field. (0/-1) |
| 4 | Reclamation Crusade | Your convoy ships' weapons gain +30cm range. VC: your convoy ships must destroy at least one enemy capital ship or six escorts. (+1/0) |
| 5 | Expeditionary Group | A convoy ship in contact with an unsurveyed celestial feature marks it surveyed (worth +50 VP each at game end). VC: at least half the features on the table are surveyed. (+1/-1) |
| 6 | Treasure Haul | Secretly nominate one convoy ship as carrying treasure; it may be captured via boarding or hit-and-run. VC: you must control the treasure ship at game end (worth 1000 VP). (+1/-2) |

**Arcs:** each bracket is one weapon mount at the listed FP or S. (L), (R) = two mounts, one per side, firing separately. (L/F/R) = one mount that can fire into any of those arcs, at one target.

### Man O' War Configuration

A Man O' War is assembled from the options below, paying both points and (where shown) Fortune; Fortune comes from the fleet commander's rating. Any weapons sharing the same range, type, and firing arc must be combined into a single weapon.

**Chassis.** Starts as a Brig (the stat row above); may take one chassis refit:

- Galleon 20pts, 2 Fortune: +2 Hits, -5cm Speed, gain a Dorsal Weapon.

- Grand Galleon 40pts, 4 Fortune: +4 Hits, -5cm Speed, gain a P/S Weapon Set.

- Ship of the Line 60pts, 6 Fortune: +6 Hits, -10cm Speed, becomes a Battleship, gains a Dorsal Weapon and a P/S Weapon Set, cannot use Come to New Heading.

**Defences.** Any of:

- (0-2) +1 Turret 10pts.

- (0-1) +1 Shield 15pts, 1 Fortune.

- (0-1) +2 Shields (10+ Hits only) 30pts, 2 Fortune.

- (0-1) 6+ Prow Armour 20pts.

**Dorsal Weapon.** If the chassis grants one, up to one of:

- Dorsal Macrobatteries 25pts (60cm FP6, L/F/R).

- Dorsal Lances 35pts (60cm FP2, L/F/R).

**Prow Weapon.** One of:

- Prow Lances (Brig only) 25pts, 1 Fortune (30cm S3, F).

- Prow Macrobatteries (non-Brig) 25pts (60cm FP6, L/F/R; not with 6+ Prow Armour).

- Prow Torps 25pts (30cm S6, F).

**P/S Weapon Sets.** Choose one, plus one more per extra set granted by the chassis; each arms both port and starboard (firing L and R). Any chassis:

- Small Macrobatteries 15pts (30cm FP6, or 45cm FP4).

- Cargo Module + Macrobatteries 20pts (+2 Transport Capacity and Fortune rating; 30cm FP4).

- (0-1) Launch Bay, 1 squadron each side, 25pts (ASF 30cm / BMR 20cm).

Galleon or larger (8+ Hits):

- (0-2) Large Macrobatteries 30pts (30cm FP10, 45cm FP6, or 60cm FP4).

- (0-1) Heavy Lances 45pts, 2 Fortune (30cm FP4; not with a Dorsal Lances).

- (0-1) Long-Range Lances 45pts, 2 Fortune (45cm FP2).

- (0-1) Launch Bays, 2 squadrons each side, 50pts (ASF 30cm / BMR 20cm).

A Man O' War with 8+ Hits may take an extra P/S weapon set for +2 Fortune (plus the chosen weapon's cost), still subject to each weapon's own limit.

**Special Refits.** Up to two total from the Imperial and/or Xenos Refits tables. The first refit in each category (Engine / Ship / Weapon) costs 1 Fortune, the second 2, the third 3, and so on, plus the usual points.

## Fleet Composition (Fleet Detachment, Expedition & Pirate Lists)

A Rogue Trader force is built with both points and Fortune. It is one of three lists: the Fleet Detachment (which joins a host fleet), the standalone Expedition Fleet, and the standalone Wolf-Pack Pirate Fleet. All three follow the Rogue Trader Special Rules above.

#### Rogue Trader Fleet Detachment (Imperium p.126)

A Fleet Detachment cannot fight alone; it joins another fleet (any except Necron or Tyranid) and is subject to the allies-of-convenience rules unless stated otherwise. Its Rogue Trader may also serve as the joined fleet's required fleet commander.

**Fleet commander, 1 Rogue Trader.** Lord Captain (Ld8) 50, Venerable Lord Captain (Ld9) 75; one free re-roll, up to two more (25/50), usable by any Rogue Trader ship or squadron. Assign to the most expensive Man O' War present (points and Fortune combined).

**Secondary, 0-3 Bond-Captains.** +1 Ld (max 8) 10pts and 1 Fortune each; assign to a Man O' War if possible, otherwise a convoy ship. Each may buy one re-roll +15 for its own ship or squadron.

**Men O' War.** Up to one per Rogue Trader and/or Bond-Captain. Each counts against the joined fleet's limits by chassis: brig as a light cruiser, galleon as a cruiser, grand galleon as a grand cruiser or battlecruiser, ship of the line as a battleship. In a Space Marine fleet, Men O' War count as strike cruisers, except grand galleons and ships of the line, which count as battle barges. Rogue Trader Man O' War: Varies.

**Convoy ships.**

- (0-1) Universe Class Mass Conveyor 200 (no more than 75% of the joined fleet's total transport capacity).

- Rogue Trader Carrack 40 (no more than one third of the joined fleet's total transport capacity).

- Convoy escorts, any number in squadrons of 2-6 (max four two-hit escorts per squadron): Escort Carrier 60 / 2 Fortune, Convoy Monitor 60 / 2 Fortune, Sloop 20, Caravel 10. Escort Carriers and Convoy Monitors are limited to one per point of the fleet's total transport capacity.

**Ordnance & upgrades.** Standard torpedoes and craft; Men O' War with torpedoes may take refitted torpedoes +20. Up to two Men O' War may swap all launch bays for mine launchers (+5 per bay), to a maximum of two mine-launcher cruisers in the whole fleet.

**Reserves & allies.** Any number of xenos cutter squadrons at 1 Fortune (1-3 ships) or 2 Fortune (4-6), points still paid, not counting as reserves. For every 750pts, one Demiurg or Kroot capital ship as a reserve (not in a Tau or Ork joined fleet). If the joined fleet includes any Space Marine ships, honour guards, or commanders, no xenos allies at all (not even cutters).

#### Rogue Trader Expedition Fleet (Imperium p.127)

A standalone Rogue Trader fleet, for when no host fleet is available or the prize is too valuable to share.

**Fleet commander, 0-1 Rogue Trader.** Lord Captain (Ld8) 50, Venerable Lord Captain (Ld9) 75; one must lead at 750+pts; one free re-roll, up to two more (25/50). Assign to the most expensive Man O' War.

**Secondary, Veteran Bond-Captains.** Up to one per two Men O' War; Ld8 50, one free re-roll for its own ship or squadron, or any escort squadron with at least one Rogue Trader convoy ship.

**Capital ships.**

- (0-1) Expedition Flagship (fleet 1000+pts; Men O' War with a grand galleon or ship-of-the-line chassis count here).

- (0-6) Expedition Cruisers (Men O' War with a brig or galleon chassis count here; the light cruisers count as Rogue Trader vessels): Rogue Trader Man O' War Varies, Dauntless LC 110, Endeavour LC 110, Siluria LC 100.

- Recommissioned Cruisers, up to one per expedition cruiser (count as Rogue Trader vessels): Lunar 180 / 1 Fortune, Carnage 180 / 1 Fortune, Tyrant 175 / 1 Fortune, Murder 170 / 1 Fortune.

- Demiurg & Kroot Mercenaries, up to one per three expedition cruisers (allies of convenience, from the Tau Empire list): Stronghold 350 / 2 Fortune, Bastion 225 / 1 Fortune, Citadel 185 / 1 Fortune, (0-1) Kroot Warsphere 145 / 1 Fortune.

**Escorts.** One squadron of 2-6 per capital ship (max four two-hit escorts): Xenos Cutter 35, plus supporting-fleet escorts (see Reserves & allies).

**Convoy ships.** (0-1) Universe Class Mass Conveyor 200 (no more than 75% of transport capacity); Carrack 40 (no more than one third of transport capacity); at least one squadron of 2-6 convoy escorts: Escort Carrier 60 / 2 Fortune, Convoy Monitor 60 / 2 Fortune, Sloop 20, Caravel 10.

**Ordnance & upgrades.** Refitted torpedoes +20; up to two capital ships may swap all launch bays for mine launchers (+5 per bay). Attack rating 3.

**Reserves & allies.** Choose a supporting fleet from another faction (not Dark Eldar, Ork, Necron, or Tyranid). Its escorts may be taken in the Escorts section above (allies of convenience; not Tau escorts needing hooks). Its capital ships may be taken as reserves at 3:1: cruisers other than grand cruisers count against the recommissioned-cruiser limit, while grand cruisers and battleships count against the expedition-flagship limit; Demiurg and Kroot cannot be taken this way. A Space Marine supporting fleet bars all xenos.

*The Expedition list prints the Bastion Commerce Vessel at 225 / 1 Fortune; its Demiurg profile in Tau & Allies shows 255. The list value is used here.*

#### Wolf-Pack Pirate Fleet (Imperium p.128-129)

A Rogue Trader list in all respects (it has a Fortune rating, and the first Pirate Captain requires a Warrant of Trade origin, treated as the pirates' origin story). Its non-Rogue-Trader classes are drawn from the Chaos list and count as Rogue Trader vessels.

**A Problem with Authority.** Rogue Trader ships suffer -1Ld while re-rolling a command check or Leadership test.

**Fleet commander, 0-3 Pirate Captains.** One per 500pts, and one must lead at 750+pts; assign to the most expensive non-xenos capital ship. Pirate Captain (Ld8) 50; one free re-roll, up to two more (25/50); a single Pirate Captain may not use more than one re-roll per turn.

**Capital ships.**

- Infamous Pirate Ships, up to one per 1000pts (grand galleon or ship-of-the-line Men O' War count here): Rogue Trader Man O' War Varies, (0-2) Fra'al Battlecruiser 250.

- Pirate Cruisers, up to one per 500pts (brig or galleon Men O' War count here; Chaos classes count as Rogue Trader vessels): Rogue Trader Man O' War Varies, Murder 170 / 1 Fortune, Slaughter 165 / 1 Fortune, (0-1) Space Marine Strike Cruiser 145 / 2 Fortune, Fanatic LC 125, Schismatic LC 125, Heretic LC 120. The strike cruiser rolls Leadership on the standard table, takes no refits from its profile or the Space Marine list, and may instead buy one upgrade each from the Imperial and/or Xenos Refits tables.

**Escorts & special ships.** At least one squadron of 2-6 per capital ship (max four two-hit escorts). Pirate escorts (Chaos classes count as Rogue Trader vessels): Escort Carrier 60 / 2 Fortune, Convoy Monitor 60 / 2 Fortune, Infidel 40, Havoc 35, Iconoclast 25, Sloop 20, Caravel 10. Pirate escort squadrons follow the convoy-ship rules for Leadership, orders, and deployment; a squadron may be exempted for 1 Fortune, letting every member (including actual convoy ships) act as normal escorts.

- Xenos escort squadrons, one squadron of 2-6 per capital ship at 1 Fortune each plus points (any mix from a faction's list, but not towed Tau or Craftworld Eldar; allies of convenience): Xenos Cutter 35, Corsair Eldar Raider Varies, Dark Eldar Raider Varies, Ork Freebooter Varies, Tau Scout Varies.

- Other xenos allies: (0-4) Stryxis Caravan Vessel 80.

**Ordnance & upgrades.** Refitted torpedoes +20; Men O' War with launch bays launch standard craft. Attack rating 3.

**Scenarios & campaigns.** May always roll on the Raids table for a scenario; if the attacker and worth 1500+pts, it may swap a rolled 6 for Planetary Assault. In a campaign it operates from a pirate base and earns extra crew skills instead of refits.

**Reserves & allies.** Apart from the options above, it neither gives nor takes ships as reserves or allies, and may not be joined by any fleet detachment.
