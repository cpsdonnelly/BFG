# Rogue Traders (Imperium p.110-129)

**Faction Rules**

*Source: Ships of the Imperium, "Rogue Trader Special Rules", printed pp.114-115.* **Fortune Rating:** the commander has a Fortune rating that works in parallel to the points limit; refits, special vessels, etc. carry a Fortune cost, and the total cannot exceed the rating. **The Warrant of Trade:** choose the dynasty's Warrant origin, which sets the base Fortune rating and special options:

| Warrant Origin | Fortune | Special Option |
|---|---|---|
| Exiled from Power | 1 | Each non-Rogue-Trader capital ship adds +1 Fortune. |
| Imperial Trade Mandate | 3 | Convoy ship Fortune increased by 50% (round down). |
| Recognition of Service | 5 | Men O' War may take no Xenos refits, but may take a third Imperial refit. |
| Cloak and Dagger Scheme | 5 | Men O' War may take no Imperial refits, but may take a third Xenos refit. |
| Ministorum Bequest | 7 | Convoy ship Fortune halved (round up). |
| Self-Promotion | 9 | Each non-Rogue-Trader capital ship costs +1 Fortune. |

**The Best That Money Can Buy.**

- *Customised Starships:* Rogue Trader ships are represented by the highly customizable Rogue Trader Man O' War.

- *Refitted Torpedoes (+20pts):* roll a D6 at game start for an extra torpedo type (in addition to any others):

**Rogue Trader Convoy Ships:** follow the normal fleet-support/convoy rules; if they disengage without scoring or counting toward victory they are treated as destroyed (crippling/destroying an enemy counts as scoring); each point of their transport capacity adds +1 Fortune. This does not apply to any generic or non-Rogue Trader vessels.

**Business as Usual:** a fleet with any Rogue Trader convoy ships rolls on the Business as Usual table (unless other scoring or victory conditions already apply to the fleet's convoy ships, such as when playing as the defender in a Convoy scenario or as the attacker in a Planetary Assault scenario), in addition to any standard sub-plot. Bracketed values are the (renown gained / lost) for meeting or failing the victory condition.

(+1/-2) |

### Man O' War Configuration

A Man O' War is assembled from the options below, paying both points and (where shown) Fortune; Fortune comes from the fleet commander's rating. Any weapons sharing the same range, type, and firing arc must be combined into a single weapon.

**Chassis.** Starts as a Brig (the stat row above); may take one chassis refit:

- Galleon 20pts, 2 Fortune: +2 Hits, -5cm Speed, gain a Dorsal Weapon.

- Grand Galleon 40pts, 4 Fortune: +4 Hits, -5cm Speed, gain a P/S Weapon Set.

- Ship of the Line 60pts, 6 Fortune: +6 Hits, -10cm Speed, becomes a Battleship, gains a Dorsal Weapon and a P/S Weapon Set, cannot use Come to New Heading.

**Defences.** Any of:

- (0-2) +1 Turret 10pts.

- (0-1) +1 Shield 15pts, 1 Fortune.

- (0-1) +2 Shields (10+ Hits only) 30pts, 2 Fortune.

- (0-1) 6+ Prow Armour 20pts.

**Dorsal Weapon.** If the chassis grants one, up to one of:

- Dorsal Macrobatteries 25pts (60cm FP6, L/F/R).

- Dorsal Lances 35pts (60cm FP2, L/F/R).

**Prow Weapon.** A man o' war may choose one of the following options:

- Prow Lances (Brig only) 25pts, 1 Fortune (30cm S3, F).

- Prow Macrobatteries (non-Brig) 25pts (60cm FP6, L/F/R; not with 6+ Prow Armour).

- Prow Torps 25pts (30cm S6, F).

**P/S Weapon Sets.** Choose one, plus one more per extra set granted by the chassis; each arms both port and starboard (firing L and R). Any chassis:

- Small Macrobatteries 15pts (30cm FP6, or 45cm FP4).

- Cargo Module + Macrobatteries 20pts (+2 Transport Capacity and Fortune rating; 30cm FP4).

- (0-1) Launch Bay, 1 squadron each side, 25pts (ASF 30cm / BMR 20cm).

Galleon or larger (8+ Hits):

- (0-2) Large Macrobatteries 30pts (30cm FP10, 45cm FP6, or 60cm FP4).

- (0-1) Heavy Lances 45pts, 2 Fortune (30cm FP4; not with a Dorsal Lances).

- (0-1) Long-Range Lances 45pts, 2 Fortune (45cm FP2).

- (0-1) Launch Bays, 2 squadrons each side, 50pts (ASF 30cm / BMR 20cm).

A Man O' War with 8+ Hits may take an extra P/S weapon set for +2 Fortune (plus the chosen weapon's cost), still subject to each weapon's own limit.

**Special Refits.** Up to two total from the Imperial and/or Xenos Refits tables. The first refit in each category (Engine / Ship / Weapon) costs 1 Fortune, the second 2, the third 3, and so on, plus the usual points.

## Fleet Composition (Fleet Detachment, Expedition & Pirate Lists)

A Rogue Trader force is built with both points and Fortune. It is one of three lists: the Fleet Detachment (which joins a host fleet), the standalone Expedition Fleet, and the standalone Wolf-Pack Pirate Fleet. All three follow the Rogue Trader Special Rules above.

#### Rogue Trader Fleet Detachment (Imperium p.126)

A Fleet Detachment cannot fight alone; it joins another fleet (any except Necron or Tyranid) and is subject to the allies-of-convenience rules unless stated otherwise. Its Rogue Trader may also serve as the joined fleet's required fleet commander.

**Fleet commander, 1 Rogue Trader.** Lord Captain (Ld8) 50, Venerable Lord Captain (Ld9) 75; one free re-roll, up to two more (25/50), usable by any Rogue Trader ship or squadron. He or she must be assigned to the most expensive man o' war present (by points and Fortune cost combined).

**Secondary, 0-3 Bond-Captains.** +1 Ld (max 8) 10pts and 1 Fortune each; assign to a Man O' War if possible, otherwise a convoy ship. Each may buy one re-roll +15 for its own ship or squadron.

**Men O' War.** Up to one per Rogue Trader and/or Bond-Captain. A man o' war counts against the joined fleet's limits based on the subtype matching its chassis (light cruiser for brigs, cruiser for galleons, grand cruiser or battlecruiser for grand galleons, and battleship for ships of the line). In a Space Marine fleet, all men o' war count as strike cruisers except grand galleons and ships of the line, which count as battle barges instead. Rogue Trader Man O' War: Varies.

**Convoy ships.**

- (0-1) Universe Class Mass Conveyor 200 (no more than 75% of the joined fleet's total transport capacity).

- Rogue Trader Carrack 40 (no more than one third of the joined fleet's total transport capacity).

- Convoy escorts, any number in squadrons of 2-6 (max four two-hit escorts per squadron): Escort Carrier 60 / 2 Fortune, Convoy Monitor 60 / 2 Fortune, Sloop 20, Caravel 10. Escort Carriers and Convoy Monitors are limited to one per point of the fleet's total transport capacity.

**Ordnance & upgrades.** Standard torpedoes and craft; Men O' War with torpedoes may take refitted torpedoes +20. Up to two Rogue Trader men o' war may replace all of their launch bays with mine launchers for +5 points per bay replaced (to a maximum of two cruisers equipped with mine launchers among the entire fleet).

**Reserves & allies.** Any number of xenos cutter squadrons at 1 Fortune (1-3 ships) or 2 Fortune (4-6), points still paid, not counting as reserves. For every 750pts, one Demiurg or Kroot capital ship as a reserve (not in a Tau or Ork joined fleet). If the joined fleet chooses to include any Space Marine ships, honour guards, or commanders, no xenos allies (not even xenos cutters) may be included. In all other respects, the joined fleet's reserve options are unchanged. Attack rating: a Rogue Trader's presence does not affect a fleet's attack rating unless otherwise specified. Scenarios & sub-plots: in a scenario that gives the joined fleet a transport capacity requirement, convoy ships from this list cannot be mixed in squadrons with those from the generic fleet support list; in any other scenario, the joined fleet must take a random Business as Usual sub-plot if it includes at least one Rogue Trader convoy ship.

#### Rogue Trader Expedition Fleet (Imperium p.127)

A standalone Rogue Trader fleet, for when no host fleet is available or the prize is too valuable to share.

**Fleet commander, 0-1 Rogue Trader.** Lord Captain (Ld8) 50, Venerable Lord Captain (Ld9) 75; one must lead at 750+pts; one free re-roll, up to two more (25/50). Assign to the most expensive Man O' War.

**Secondary, Veteran Bond-Captains.** Up to one per two Men O' War; Ld8 50, one free re-roll for its own ship or squadron, or any escort squadron with at least one Rogue Trader convoy ship.

**Capital ships.**

- (0-1) Expedition Flagship (fleet 1000+pts; Men O' War with a grand galleon or ship-of-the-line chassis count here).

- (0-6) Expedition Cruisers (Men O' War with a brig or galleon chassis count here; the light cruisers count as Rogue Trader vessels): Rogue Trader Man O' War Varies, Dauntless LC 110, Endeavour LC 110, Siluria LC 100.

- Recommissioned Cruisers, up to one per expedition cruiser (count as Rogue Trader vessels): Lunar 180 / 1 Fortune, Carnage 180 / 1 Fortune, Tyrant 175 / 1 Fortune, Murder 170 / 1 Fortune.

- Demiurg & Kroot Mercenaries, up to one per three expedition cruisers (allies of convenience, from the Tau Empire list): Stronghold 350 / 2 Fortune, Bastion 225 / 1 Fortune, Citadel 185 / 1 Fortune, (0-1) Kroot Warsphere 145 / 1 Fortune.

**Escorts.** One squadron of 2-6 per capital ship (max four two-hit escorts): Xenos Cutter 35, plus supporting-fleet escorts (see Reserves & allies).

**Convoy ships.** (0-1) Universe Class Mass Conveyor 200 (no more than 75% of transport capacity); Carrack 40 (no more than one third of transport capacity); at least one squadron of 2-6 convoy escorts: Escort Carrier 60 / 2 Fortune, Convoy Monitor 60 / 2 Fortune, Sloop 20, Caravel 10.

**Ordnance & upgrades.** Rogue Trader ships with torpedo tubes are armed with ordinary torpedoes; any torpedo-armed Rogue Trader capital ship may buy refitted torpedoes +20; Men O' War with launch bays launch standard fighters and/or bombers; up to two capital ships may swap all launch bays for mine launchers (+5 per bay). Attack rating 3. Sub-plots: when playing any scenario that does not have its own transport capacity requirement, the fleet must take a random Business as Usual sub-plot if it includes any convoy ships.

**Reserves & allies.** Choose a supporting fleet from another faction (not Dark Eldar, Ork, Necron, or Tyranid). Its escorts may be taken in the Escorts section above (allies of convenience; not Tau escorts needing hooks). Its capital ships may be taken as reserves at 3:1: cruisers other than grand cruisers count against the recommissioned-cruiser limit, while grand cruisers and battleships count against the expedition-flagship limit; Demiurg and Kroot cannot be taken this way. A Space Marine supporting fleet bars all xenos.

*The Expedition list prints the Bastion Commerce Vessel at 225 / 1 Fortune; its Demiurg profile in Tau & Allies shows 255. The list value is used here.*

#### Wolf-Pack Pirate Fleet (Imperium p.128-129)

A Rogue Trader list in all respects (it has a Fortune rating, and the first Pirate Captain requires a Warrant of Trade origin, treated as the pirates' origin story). The non-Rogue Trader classes below are found in the Chaos fleet list, and will count as Rogue Trader ships in all respects.

**A Problem with Authority.** Rogue Trader ships in the fleet suffer a -1Ld penalty while re-rolling a command check or leadership test.

**Fleet commander, 0-3 Pirate Captains.** One per 500pts (or portion thereof), and one must lead at 750+pts; assign to the most expensive capital ship(s) available (excluding xenos ships). Pirate Captain (Ld8) 50; one free re-roll, up to two more (25/50); a single Pirate Captain may not use more than one re-roll per turn.

**Capital ships.**

- Infamous Pirate Ships, up to one per 1000pts (or portion thereof) (grand galleon or ship-of-the-line Men O' War count here): Rogue Trader Man O' War Varies, (0-2) Fra'al Battlecruiser 250.

- Pirate Cruisers, up to one per 500pts (or portion thereof) (brig or galleon Men O' War count here; Chaos classes count as Rogue Trader vessels): Rogue Trader Man O' War Varies, Murder 170 / 1 Fortune, Slaughter 165 / 1 Fortune, (0-1) Space Marine Strike Cruiser 145 / 2 Fortune, Fanatic LC 125, Schismatic LC 125, Heretic LC 120. The strike cruiser rolls Leadership on the standard table, takes no refits from its profile or the Space Marine list, and may instead buy one upgrade each from the Imperial and/or Xenos Refits tables.

**Escorts & special ships.** At least one squadron of 2-6 per capital ship (max four two-hit escorts). Pirate escorts (Chaos classes count as Rogue Trader vessels): Escort Carrier 60 / 2 Fortune, Convoy Monitor 60 / 2 Fortune, Infidel 40, Havoc 35, Iconoclast 25, Sloop 20, Caravel 10. Pirate escort squadrons follow the convoy-ship rules for Leadership, orders, and deployment; a squadron may be exempted for 1 Fortune, letting every member (including actual convoy ships) act as normal escorts.

- Xenos escort squadrons, one squadron of 2-6 per capital ship at 1 Fortune each plus points (any mix from a faction's list, but not towed Tau or Craftworld Eldar; allies of convenience): Xenos Cutter 35, Corsair Eldar Raider Varies, Dark Eldar Raider Varies, Ork Freebooter Varies, Tau Scout Varies.

- Other xenos allies: (0-4) Stryxis Caravan Vessel 80.

**Ordnance & upgrades.** Pirate ships with torpedo tubes are armed with ordinary torpedoes; any torpedo-armed capital ship may buy refitted torpedoes +20; Men O' War with launch bays launch standard fighters and bombers. Attack rating 3. Sub-plots: when playing any scenario that does not have its own transport capacity requirement, the fleet must take a random Business as Usual sub-plot if it includes any convoy ships.

**Scenarios & campaigns.** May always roll on the Raids table for a scenario; if the attacker and worth 1500+pts, it may swap a rolled 6 for Planetary Assault. In a campaign, a pirate fleet always operates from a pirate base, and earns extra crew skills rather than earning any refits.

**Reserves & allies.** Apart from the options above, it neither gives nor takes ships as reserves or allies, and may not be joined by any fleet detachment.

### High-Risk Convoys (Imperium p.129)

The threat tables below may be used as alternatives to the one presented in the Convoy Run scenario. If used, the defending player may add up to +50 points or +2 Fortune rating to their fleet's limit for every 2 points of transport capacity required. While these alternate tables are well-suited to Rogue Traders and pirates, there is absolutely no reason why they cannot be used in one-off games or campaigns between any two fleets, regardless of their respective factions.

**The Cruiser Trap.** This table represents a stronger force of attacking capital ships being in the area the convoy is traversing.

| D6 | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 | Two deadfall torpedo or attack craft clusters. |
| 2 | Three deadfall torpedo or attack craft clusters (in any mix desired). |
| 3 | One squadron of escort ships worth up to 150 points. |
| 4 | One capital ship worth up to 185 points. |
| 5 | One capital ship worth up to 210 points. |
| 6 | One capital ship worth up to 250 points. |

**Stalkers of the Abyss.** This table represents the dangers of passing through a dark and frightening corner of the galaxy.

| D6 | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 | One daemon mine cluster. |
| 2 | Two daemon mine clusters. |
| 3 | 2D6 Warp Beasts! These use the profile and rules of Behemoth protoforms (see the Tyranids faction list in Ships of the Xenos). They are armed with feeder tendrils and operate as a squadron. |
| 4 | Giant Warp Beast! This uses the profile and rules of a Carnivore bioship (see the Tyranids faction list in Ships of the Xenos). It is armed with only dorsal massive claws and keel feeder tendrils (instead of its usual set of weapons). |
| 5 | Terror Beast! This is treated as a Defence with 4 Hits, 6+ Armour, 2 Shields, and 2 Turrets. It has no weapons and must move exactly 10cm in any direction on each of its turns. If it contacts a ship (friend or foe), both it and the other ship vanish! The 'abducted' ship counts as being lost in the warp (see the rules for Warp Rifts). The Terror Beast reappears in D3 turns: place it at least 20cm away from any other ships at the start of the movement phase. |
| 6 | One Chaos daemonship of the attacker's choice worth up to 250 points (including all upgrades). |

When activated, daemon mine clusters are replaced by D3+2 daemon mines, which function as orbital mines in all respects except that they move an extra D6cm each time they move, and they have a 4+ resilient save. Each is worth 10 victory points when destroyed for any reason.

Warp beasts must attack the closest defending ship in range, cannot use any special orders (including Brace For Impact), and automatically pass all leadership tests they must make against celestial phenomena. Each warp beast or terror beast rolled on the table above is worth a flat 100 victory points.
