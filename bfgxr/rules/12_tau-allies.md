# Tau & Allies (Xenos p.77-101)

## Faction Rules

*Source: Ships of the Xenos, "Tau Empire Special Rules", printed pp.77-78; ally rules from the Tau fleet lists, printed pp.95-101.*

**Ship Systems.**

- *Ion Cannons:* function as lance type weapons.

- *Railguns: function as gunnery* type weapons like macrobatteries.

- *Gravitic Launchers:* like torpedo launchers but always fire Tau missiles.

- *Gravitic Hooks:* each hook tows one escort of a set class (a fleet may not field more towed escorts than matching hooks); reconfiguration must be done equally for all hooks; otherwise no in-game effect (ships do not start docked with them and cannot re-dock during the game) and not counted as weapons for criticals.

- *Deflectors:* a prow shield system giving the front facing a 6+ Armour value against all attacks to that facing; disabled by a prow critical (restored when repaired); does not count as shields.

- *Tracking Systems:* any Tau vessel within 10cm of a ship/station with tracking systems may re-roll missed turret attacks and ignores the right column-shift for gunnery beyond 30cm (works even while the source is on Brace).

**Ordnance.** Barracuda fighters (speed 25cm) are normal fighters. Manta missile destroyers are bombers with a 4+ resilient save (speed 20cm). Tiger Shark drone carriers are torpedo bombers producing missile salvos, 4+ resilient (speed 20cm). *Tau Missiles:* use torpedo markers but may move 20-40cm each ordnance phase (player's choice) and make one 45° turn at the start of their move; unreliable drives mean at the start of each ordnance phase you roll 1D6 per point of strength for each previously-launched salvo, and each 1 reduces its strength by 1.

**Boarding & Hit-and-Run:** Tau Kor'vattra vessels always halve their troop rating (rounding up) and cannot conduct teleport attacks. Kor'or'vesh vessels suffer no penalty to their troop rating, but still cannot conduct teleport attacks. (These penalties apply only to Tau vessels.)

### Tau Allies

**Nicassar.**

- *Natural Spacefarers:* all Nicassar stations and squadrons +1 starting Ld (max 10).

- *Frail Crews:* never initiate boarding or teleport; halve troop rating (round up) when boarded. Dhows, rigs, and caravans are separate units that cannot link or break up mid-battle (swappable in a campaign).

**Demiurg (War Host).** Field no fleet commanders; may instead buy up to two command-check re-rolls usable by any Demiurg vessel (one 25 points, two 75 points); see the Demiurg War Host Fleet List below. Use only generic attack craft and ordinary torpedoes. The fleet must contain more Demiurg commerce vessels than all other non-Demiurg cruisers combined. In campaigns they progress like an Imperial Navy fleet.

Tau weapon and system terms follow the Tau faction rules: Railguns = macrobatteries, Ion Cannon = lance, Gravitic Launcher = missile launcher. Armr "6+D / 5+" means a prow Deflector (6+ front, disabled by a prow critical) over 5+ armour. Tracking Systems help nearby Tau ships; Gravitic Hooks each tow one escort of a set class. Attack craft: Barracuda Fighters (ASF, 25cm) and Manta Bombers (BMR, 20cm); some ships may swap Mantas for Tiger Shark drone carriers. Tau weapon arcs are often the narrow L/F and R/F.

**Arcs:** each bracket is one weapon mount at the listed FP or S. (L), (R) = two mounts, one per side, firing separately. (L/F/R) = one mount that can fire into any of those arcs, at one target. (L/F), (R/F) = two mounts whose arcs overlap at the front.

**Tau Civilian Orbitals** (planetary-defence choices). Each has exactly one Core Module plus up to four optional modules (defence, habitat, or manufacturing & research); any optional module may be taken more than once. The whole assembly is one model, and its cost is the core plus all attached modules.

**Core Module** 20pts: Defence/4, 1 Shield, 5+, 2 Turrets (command, comms, energy, sensors).

**Defence Module** (adds +1 Turret and one armament): Railguns +15pts; Ion Cannon +15pts; Launch Bay +10pts (1 squadron of Barracudas or Mantas; +5pts to swap Mantas for Tiger Sharks); Gravitic Hook +5pts (+1 Orca/Warden/Dhow fleet capacity).

**Manufacturing & Research Module** 10pts: +1 Shield to the core (max 3), and grants tracking systems (+5cm range per module after the first, max 20cm).

**Habitat Module** 5pts: +1 to the core module's starting hits per module (crew and living space, plus ablative armour or decoy sections).

**Tau Waystations** are a civilian orbital of exactly one Core Module plus one Defence Module of choice. Waystations may be included in any scenario where the Tau are defenders, even if planetary defences would not normally be allowed (although they will count against your fleet's points limit in such cases). Tau waystations always count as having tracking systems for no extra cost. Otherwise they follow the civilian orbital rules above.

**Allies: Demiurg**

Demiurg commerce vessels share several traits: Ponderous (no Come to New Heading); heavily automated, starting each battle at a fixed Ld rather than rolling; an armoured 6+ prow; Absorber/Repulsor Fields (they never suffer a speed penalty for being in contact with blast markers, are immune to the effects of solar flares, radiation bursts, and gas/dust clouds, and never gain blast markers due to any type of celestial phenomena; when a Demiurg vessel moves, it must collect every blast marker touched by its base during its movement, removing them from the table and setting them aside in a separate pile for that vessel; markers in contact when it begins its move are collected immediately and so do not affect its leadership if it attempts a command check, but command checks made at other times are still affected normally); and, in two specific circumstances only, a higher victory-point value when destroyed (see Demiurg VP below). Each commerce vessel begins each battle with a nominal Ld value (shown in its notes) rather than rolling randomly. For each point of damage it suffers thereafter, it reduces its Ld value by 1 for the remainder of the battle, to a minimum of Ld5. If it suffers a 'Bridge Smashed!' critical result, it suffers one extra point of damage rather than the usual -3 Ld penalty. The **Cutting Beam** is a lance (15cm, Front): base firepower 1, +1 per blast marker the ship collected in its preceding movement phase, to a maximum of firepower 8 (shown as FP1+). Alternatively, two markers may be expended to increase the cutting beam's range to 30cm (with the rest contributing to its firepower). Unused markers are lost at the end of each turn. Demiurg attack craft are Fighters (ASF, 30cm), Bombers (BMR, 20cm), and Assault Boats (AB, 30cm). Mercenaries: unless a fleet is composed entirely of Demiurg vessels, they are considered to be hired mercenaries. In this case, Demiurg ships will not continue to fight after becoming crippled, instead attempting to disengage each and every turn thereafter, or moving as fast as possible towards the nearest table edge if they fail, until they have successfully left the board. However, such is the Demiurg's hatred for the Orks that if the opposing fleet includes any Ork vessels, Demiurg ships will not begin attempting to disengage until they have 2 or fewer hit points remaining, rather than when they are crippled. Hired Reserves: any fleet except Orks, Tyranids, and Necrons may substitute Bastion or Citadel commerce vessels in place of any reserve cruisers it is normally allowed. They follow the normal rules for reserves and allies of convenience, and are still subject to the Mercenaries rule.

**Demiurg VP.** A commerce vessel is normally worth its points cost, like any other ship. It is worth the higher value **only** when either: the fleet is composed **entirely** of Demiurg ships, **or** the opposing fleet includes **any** Ork vessels. Stronghold 350 → 400, Bastion 255 → 300, Citadel 185 → 200. *(Xenos p.92-94.)*

Of the three, only the Dhow is a towed escort (towed by Tau gravitic hooks). Nicassar Caravans must move exactly 10cm in any direction during each of their movement phases.

### Tau Ship Special Rules

#### Kor'O Vash'ya Y'eldi Mesme (Xenos p.99-100)

Any Tau Kor'or'vesh / Kor'vattra fleet may be led by O'Mesme (Ld9) for 110pts instead of a regular Kor'el or Kor'O.

**Aun'Shear:** accompanied by the Ethereal Aun'Shear, he comes with two free fleet-commander re-rolls instead of one.

**Inspired Pilots:** his ship gains the Superior Pilots crew skill (*Xenos p.122*).

**Superior Command Systems:** his ship always counts as having Improved Logic Engines (*Xenos p.123*).

**Personal Flagship:** a fleet led by O'Mesme may include (0-1) The Se'arle O'Mesme 400, a unique Custodian class battleship equipped with Tiger Shark drone carriers in addition to its usual attack craft. It counts as one of the fleet's allowed Custodians, and O'Mesme must be embarked aboard it if it is taken.

### Kroot Ship Special Rules

#### Kroot Warsphere (Xenos p.98)

Classed as a defence (for gunnery, ramming, and planetary-defence purposes). Its movement and damage rules are identical to the Ork Rok; both auto-pass All Ahead Full (the Rok via the Ork 'Ere We Go! rule, the Warsphere via its omni-directional thrusters):

**Landed:** a Warsphere in low orbit that contacts the planetary edge counts as landed (disengaged, not destroyed). In a Planetary Assault scenario, each attacking Warsphere that successfully lands on the planet automatically scores +3 assault points; Warspheres may never score assault points by any other means.

**Troops:** double troop rating (max 20) before other modifiers.

**Movement:** moves exactly 10cm straight each turn; may not turn or use Burn Retros or Come to New Heading, but automatically passes command checks for All Ahead Full (omni-directional thrusters). On All Ahead Full it moves an extra 2D6cm in any direction at the end of its move; if that shifts it 10cm or more in a new direction, that becomes its heading (a normal ship's All Ahead Full only extends along its current heading). Crippled Warspheres and Warspheres in blast markers do not slow. Warspheres in the gravity well of a planet or moon may make free turns like an ordinary ship (45°) and/or place themselves in a stationary or low orbit.

**Damage:** Each critical hit instead inflicts +1 point of damage, in the same way as an escort ship or small defence. At 0 damage it breaks up with no Catastrophic Damage roll: replace it with four blast markers.

**Fleet Composition (Tau Empire Fleet List)**

One Empire list with two divisions, the Kor'or'vesh (Protector Fleet) and Kor'vattra (Merchant Fleet), plus allied contingents (*Xenos p.99-101*). **Fleet commander (0-1 Kor Caste Flag Officer):** Kor'el (Ld8) 50, Kor'O (Ld9) 70; if the fleet is worth more than 750 points, one must be included to lead it, and if a Custodian is present the fleet must be led by a Kor'O (which may be Kor'O'Mesme). One free fleet commander re-roll (Kor'O'Mesme comes with two), and up to two more (one 25 points, two 50 points). **Named officer:** the unique Kor'O Vash'ya Y'eldi Mesme (Ld9) 110 comes with two free re-rolls and brings his flagship, (0-1) The Se'arle O'Mesme 400 (a unique Custodian).
