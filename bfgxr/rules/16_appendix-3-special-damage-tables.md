# Appendix 3 - Special Damage Tables

*Critical hits and catastrophic damage for the Ramilies Star Fort, the Fortress-Monastery, the Chaos and Ork Space Hulks, Eldar and Necron vessels, and space stations and other high orbit defences with 3 or more starting hits. Everything else uses Appendix 5, p.204.*

### Imperial Navy

### Star Fort / Fortress-Monastery Critical Hits Table

*Used by the Ramilies Star Fort (Imperial Navy) and the Fortress-Monastery (Space Marines). Roll 2D6; results apply to the quadrant hit unless stated. Source: Imperium p.88 / p.147.*

| 2D6 | Extra Damage | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 2-3 | +0 | **Lances damaged.** The quadrant's lance array is offline until repaired. |
| 4 | +0 | **Macrobatteries damaged.** The quadrant's weapons batteries fire at half-strength until the damage has been repaired. |
| 5 | +0 | **Launch bays damaged.** The quadrant cannot launch ordnance until repaired. |
| 6 | +1 | **Reactors damaged.** The quadrant's shields and turrets are at half-strength until the damage has been repaired. |
| 7 | +0 | **Fire!** Roll to repair (extinguish) the fire in the end phase; if it is not put out, the fire causes 1 point of extra damage and continues to burn. |
| 8 | +D3 | **Hull breach.** A gash is torn in the quadrant's hull. |
| 9 | +0 | **Command tower destroyed.** Leadership is reduced by 2 (cannot be repaired). |
| 10 | +0 | **Shields collapse.** The quadrant's shield strength drops to zero (cannot be repaired). |
| 11 | +D3 | **Basilica breached.** All quadrants suffer the +D3 extra damage and any basilica weapons are lost (cannot be repaired). |
| 12 | +D6 | **Reactor struck.** All quadrants suffer the +D6 extra damage. Roll again on this table and apply the result to the appropriate quadrant. |

**Star Fort / Fortress-Monastery Catastrophic Damage Table**

*Roll D6 when all quadrants reach 0 hits. Source: Imperium p.88 / p.147.*

| D6 | Result |
|---|---|
| 1-2 | **Wrecked.** The station stops functioning; venting gases and debris block line of sight across the fort, and movement through it counts as moving through an asteroid field in all respects. |
| 3 | **Structural collapse.** Replace the model with a 15cm diameter asteroid field. |
| 4-5 | **Hyper-plasma meltdown.** Resolve 8 lance shots against all ships within 4D6cm and remove all ordnance in range; everything else on the table suffers a solar flare centred on the fort. Replace the fort with 2D6 blast markers. |
| 6 | **Warp field implosion.** Resolve four lance shots against all ships and ordnance within 4D6cm, then replace the station with a warp rift. All remaining ships and ordnance markers are drawn 15cm toward the rift's centre and are affected as per the Celestial Phenomena rules if they enter it. |

### Chaos

**Space Hulk Critical Hits Table**

*Used by the Chaos Space Hulk and the Ork Space Hulk. Roll D6 when a hit would be a critical (re-roll any result that cannot be applied). Source: Imperium p.45 / Xenos p.70.*

| D6 | Result |
|---|---|
| 1-2 | **Target high-energy systems.** Roll again: 1-2 reduce Turrets by 1; 3-4 reduce Shields by 1; 5-6 reduce the firepower of all lances by 1. |
| 3-4 | **Target weapons clusters.** The attacker chooses one of the following (applicable) options: reduce the firepower of a gunnery weapon facing the attacker by 2; reduce the strength of a torpedo salvo (a *launcha*, for Orks) facing the attacker by 2; or reduce the launch capacity of any launch bay by 2. |
| 5+ | **Target thruster assemblies.** Note each time this is rolled. Each time the hulk wishes to turn, it must roll higher on a D6 than the number of thruster assembly critical hits it has suffered during the course of the battle. This does not include the free turn it may make when it is within a planet's gravity well. Maximum 6 such hits. |

### Eldar

**Eldar Critical Hits Table**

*Used by all Corsair and Craftworld Eldar vessels. Roll 2D6. Eldar take critical hits on a 5+ (Fragile; Ghostships on 6+). Source: MMS p.5.*

| 2D6 | Extra Damage | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | +0 | **Infinity circuit damaged.** The ship's infinity circuit, which aids control and internal communications, is damaged by the hit. The ship's Leadership is reduced by 1 until the damage can be repaired. If there are multiples of this critical hit, Leadership is reduced by 1 for each hit. |
| 3 | +0 | **Keel armament damaged.** The keel armament is taken offline by the hit and may not fire until it has been repaired. |
| 4 | +0 | **Prow armament damaged.** The ship's prow is ripped open. Its prow armament may not fire until it has been repaired. A second critical hit to the prow will destroy the ship's port and starboard weaponry if it has any; both are destroyed with the same result and likewise repaired. |
| 5 | +0 | **Mast lines severed.** The systems that allow the ship to alter the angle of the sails and turn swiftly are broken by the hit. Until the damage is repaired, the ship may make no turns in its movement phase. |
| 6 | +0 | **Mainsail scarred.** The ship's main solar sail suffers surface damage, reducing the amount of energy it can store. Each of the ship's speeds is reduced by 5cm until the sail is repaired. |
| 7 | +1 | **Superstructure damaged.** The hit tears into the ship, causing a small breach. Until the damage is repaired, whenever the ship turns in its movement phase it must roll a 3+ to avoid suffering a hit. |
| 8 | +0 | **Mainsail shredded.** The solar cells of the mainsail are torn to tatters by the hit. The ship only gets one move per movement phase until the damage is repaired. |
| 9 | +1 | **Holofield generators destroyed.** The holofield generators are smashed beyond repair by the hit. The ship no longer benefits from its holofields. This damage may not be repaired. |
| 10 | +1 | **Shields collapse.** The shield generators overload and burn out, leaving the ship virtually defenceless. The ship's shield strength is reduced to zero. This damage may not be repaired. |
| 11 | +D3 | **Hull breach.** A huge gash is torn in the ship's hull, causing carnage among the crew. |
| 12 | +D6 | **Bulkhead collapse.** Internal pillars buckle and twist; whole compartments crumple with a psychic scream of tortured wraithbone. |

### Necrons

**Necron Critical Hits Table**

*Used by all Necron vessels. Roll 2D6. Leadership penalties cannot be removed during the game, even if the system is repaired. Source: Xenos p.41.*

| 2D6 | Extra Damage | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | +0 | **Port power relays damaged.** The ship's port power relays are taken offline by the hit, preventing its lightning arc or particle whip from firing at targets in its port arc until the damage has been repaired. |
| 3 | +0 | **Starboard power relays damaged.** The ship's starboard power relays are taken offline by the hit, preventing its lightning arc or particle whip from firing at targets in its starboard arc until the damage has been repaired. |
| 4 | +0 | **Main capacitors damaged.** The ship's primary solar capacitors are taken offline by the hit, preventing its lightning arc from firing at all until the damage has been repaired. |
| 5 | +0 | **Power flow disrupted.** The ship's power relays automatically shut down to avoid internal damage, preventing its lightning arc or particle whip from firing at all until the damage has been repaired. |
| 6 | -1 Ld | **Main drive damaged.** The ship's main propulsion and control system is damaged by the hit. The ship may not turn until the damage has been repaired. |
| 7 | -1 Ld | **Impulse power disrupted.** The power relays to the ship's drive systems are taken offline by the hit. Reduce the ship's speed by 5cm until the damage has been repaired. |
| 8 | -1 Ld | **Inertialess drive damaged.** The ship's inertialess drive system is taken offline by the hit, rendering it unable to counteract its own enormous mass. The ship may not use *All Ahead Full* special orders until the damage has been repaired. |
| 9 | -2 Ld | **Monument damaged.** The ship's monument is struck by a direct hit and taken offline. Its fleet commander re-rolls and nightmare field (if any) cannot be used until the damage has been repaired. |
| 10 | -3 Ld | **Command core damaged.** The ship's central command core suffers serious damage, inhibiting its ability to control key systems. |
| 11 | +D3 / -2 Ld | **Power surge.** The ship is rocked by a massive surge of energy, overloading relays and fusing control circuits across its length. |
| 12 | +D6 / -2 Ld | **Power core ruptured.** A huge ball of incandescent energy spews forth from the breached core, liquefying internal structures and severing control to vast areas of the ship. Retreat is advisable. |

### Fleet Support

**Space Station Critical Hits Table**

*Used by high orbit defences with 3 or more starting hits. Roll 2D6. Source: BFG:XR Rulebook, "Fleet Support", p.62.*

| 2D6 | Extra Damage | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 2-3 | +0 | **Lances damaged.** The station may not fire its lances until repaired. |
| 4 | +0 | **Main armament damaged.** The station's weapon batteries are taken offline by the hit and may not fire until the damage has been repaired. |
| 5 | +0 | **Ordnance bays hit.** The station may not launch any ordnance until the damage has been repaired, and its bays do not count towards the fleet's launch capacity. |
| 6 | +1 | **Reactors damaged.** Until power is restored, the station's shields and turrets values are halved, rounding up. |
| 7 | +0 | **Fire!** Each fire that the station fails to repair (extinguish) in the end phase inflicts 1 point of damage on it. |
| 8-9 | +1 | **Orbit lost.** The station falls D6cm towards the planet's marker in each of its movement phases until either the damage is repaired or it hits the marker and is destroyed. |
| 10 | +0 | **Shields collapse.** Shield strength reduced to zero (cannot be repaired). |
| 11 | +D3 | **Hull breach.** |
| 12 | +D6 | **Bulkhead collapse.** |
