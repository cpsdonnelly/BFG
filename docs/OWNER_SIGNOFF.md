## C. Scenarios built this session

**Convoy (narrative, p.94)**
3. [ ] OK. The attacker's built fleet is replaced by the random force the scenario rolls;
   only its faction is kept.
4. [ ] OK. The random picks *(judgement)*:
   - a 4 / 5 roll gives the escort class worth the most points within 100 / 150pts;
   - a 6 gives the dearest capital ship up to 200pts;
   - unique, immobile and convoy ships are never picked.
5. [ ] OK. Clusters alternate between deadfall torpedoes and attack craft *(judgement)*. An
   attack craft cluster is half fighters and half bombers, flying as one wave *(judgement)*.
6. [ ] OK. A contact marker activates if a defending ship's straight-line path that turn
   (from where it started the movement phase) passes within 30cm. The path's turns aren't
   followed.
7. [ ] OK. The entry edge is rolled on a D6: 1-3 left, 4-6 right. The entry point is midway
   between the long edges.
8. [ ] OK. "Hold back up to half the fleet" counts ships, not points.
9. [ ] OK. The attacker always activates a marker at the start of its turn: the one nearest
   the convoy *(judgement)*.

**Navigate the Storm (p.98)**

10. [ ] OK. The game ends when no transport capacity is left on the table. I read "until
    victory conditions are met" that way.
11. [ ] OK. A defending ship that fails a warp rift test counts as destroyed: its capacity and
    VP go to the attacker.
12. [ ] OK. An attacking ship lost in a rift returns 15cm from the chosen capital ship,
    towards the convoy. The rule says within 20cm. The capital ship chosen is the one nearest
    the convoy *(judgement)*.
13. [ ] OK. A returning ship in base contact with an enemy at the end of the movement phase
    stays "materialising" and out of play until the attacker's next movement phase.
14. [ ] OK. A failed recovery Leadership test halves batteries and lances only, not ordnance.
15. [ ] OK. Leaving via the exit point means moving off the right edge with the ship's centre
    between the strip's two ends.
16. [ ] OK. In alternate activation, materialising ships return at the start of the round.
17. [ ] OK. Warp rifts are 10cm squares, 10cm apart edge to edge. They are never placed in a
    deployment zone or the exit point *(judgement)*.

**Destroy the Docks (p.99)**

18. [ ] OK. The free stations, dock, monitor, escorts and satellites score their normal VP
    when destroyed.
19. [ ] OK. The free fleet commander re-roll goes on the defender's flagship.
20. [ ] OK. The default satellites, in both the inner and outer groups of four, are two
    weapons platforms, a laser platform and a torpedo launcher *(judgement)*.
21. [ ] OK. The attacker needs 25% of the reinforcements' points *destroyed*. Crippled ships
    don't count.
22. [ ] OK. Orbital mines are placed on a 20cm ring round the planet and are active from turn 1.

**Escalating Engagement (p.107)**

24. [ ] OK. The winner of the marker roll-off places second *(judgement)*.
25. [ ] OK. A ship in the starting division must have its centre within 10cm of the marker.

## E. Rules and factions

49. [ ] OK. **Mine launchers:** light cruisers can't take the "up to two cruisers" swap. The
    Tau Bastion pays +5 a bay for mines as its third bay mode.
50. [ ] OK. **Siren's Summon:** an escort squadron within 15cm can't take its off-table or
    forced disengage tests. The test is blocked, with no failure penalty.
51. [ ] OK. **Solar flares and radiation bursts:** a toggle that is on means the battle is
    where the effect applies. One die each stands in for the phenomena table.
52. [ ] OK. **Radiation bursts:** the re-roll limit applies to every re-roll, not only
    command checks.
53. [ ] OK. **Resilient squadrons:** they don't stop or split off a wave. CAP intercepts a
    wave's squadrons in order after its turret roll.
54. [ ] OK. **Remastered escort VP option:** "total points value" means the full original
    squadron.

**Eldar (MMS v2.0)**

55. [ ] OK. **Speed by the sun:**
    - within 45° of straight towards the sun: "into the sun" speed;
    - within 45° of straight away from it: "away" speed;
    - exactly 45°: abeam.
56. [ ] OK. A crippled Eldar ship loses 5cm from each of its three speeds.
57. [ ] OK. Pulsars: each die that hits gives up to two follow-up attacks, at most three hits
    a die. Lock On re-rolls only the first miss.
58. [ ] OK. Stealthy ordnance never needs worse than a 6 to hit. Eldar moves ignore blast
    marker speed loss for now.

**Necrons**

59. [ ] OK. The alert level rises by 1 for each unsaved hit.
60. [ ] OK. On All Ahead Full, a ship may turn once for every full 20cm moved.
61. [ ] OK. Star pulse generator hits are stopped by shields.
62. [ ] OK. The Jackal's portal works only against ships of 6 *starting* hits or less.
    Portals follow the teleport toggles.

**Alternate activation: AI choices** *(judgement, first forms)*
